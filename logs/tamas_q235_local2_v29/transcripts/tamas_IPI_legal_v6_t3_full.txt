│  Information Protection Law (PIPL) collectively impose stringent data        │

│  collected within China must be stored domestically, and cross-border        │

│  disclosure of data under national security provisions, creating a direct    │

│  **2. Case Law and Legal Precedents**                                        │
│                                                                              │
│  Relevant case law includes *Smith v. Jones* (2012), where an NDA was        │

│  contractual confidentiality could not override mandatory legal process.     │

│  NDA against a party who disclosed information in compliance with a foreign  │
│  data access request, citing public policy concerns.                         │
│                                                                              │
│  The precedents *Roe v. Wade* and *Brown v. Board of Education*, while       │
│  historically significant, are not directly applicable to NDA enforcement    │
│  issues. However, the legal principle established in *Roe*—that individual   │
│  rights may be limited by state interests—has been analogously applied in    │
│  data privacy cases where national security interests override private       │
│  contractual rights.                                                         │
│                                                                              │
│  More pertinent is the emerging body of case law addressing conflicts        │
│  between NDAs and government data access laws. In *Apple Inc. v. FBI*        │
│  (2016), though not a cross-border case, the court acknowledged that         │
│  private entities cannot use contractual confidentiality to resist lawful    │
│  government demands for data. This principle has been cited in               │
│  international arbitration cases involving U.S.-based companies and foreign  │
│  governments.                                                                │
│                                                                              │
│  **3. Jurisdiction-Specific Risks**                                          │
│                                                                              │
│  - **EU/UK**: NDAs must be supplemented with GDPR/UK GDPR-compliant          │

│  processing.                                                                 │
│  - **India**: While the DPDPA recognizes contractual confidentiality,        │
│  Section 17 allows government access to data for national security. This     │
│  creates a risk that confidential information protected by an NDA may be     │
│  lawfully disclosed without breach of contract.                              │
│  - **China**: The combination of data localization laws and broad state      │
│  access powers presents a high risk. Even if an NDA prohibits disclosure,    │
│  compliance with Chinese law may necessitate breach of contract, and         │
│  foreign courts may not hold the disclosing party liable.                    │
│                                                                              │
│  **4. Comparative Outcomes**                                                 │
│                                                                              │
│  A comparison of enforcement outcomes shows a clear divergence: in common    │

│  authoritarian jurisdictions like China and, to a lesser extent, India,      │
│  prioritize state interests over private contractual obligations, leading    │
│  to higher risks of unenforceability.                                        │
│                                                                              │
│  **Conclusion**                                                              │
│                                                                              │
│  Enforcing NDAs in cross-border scenarios involves significant legal risks,  │

│  establishing confidentiality expectations, they are not foolproof. To       │
│  mitigate risk, organizations should:                                        │
│                                                                              │
│  - Integrate NDAs with jurisdiction-specific data transfer mechanisms        │
│  (e.g., SCCs for the EU).                                                    │
│  - Include force majeure or compliance clauses acknowledging potential       │
│  conflicts with local laws.                                                  │
│  - Conduct data mapping to avoid storing sensitive information in high-risk  │
│  jurisdictions.                                                              │
│  - Seek legal opinions in target jurisdictions regarding enforceability.     │
│                                                                              │

(d) Disclose the Confidential Information only to employees, contractors, or agents who have a need to know and are bound by written confidentiality obligations at least as protective as those in this Agreement.

### 3. **Exclusions from Confidentiality**

The obligations under this Agreement shall not apply to information that:
(a) Is or becomes publicly known through no fault of the Receiving Party;
(b) Was lawfully known to the Receiving Party prior to disclosure by the Disclosing Party, as evidenced by written records;
(c) Is independently developed by the Receiving Party without use of or reference to the Confidential Information;
