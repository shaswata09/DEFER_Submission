**EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT**

This Employment Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of April 5, 2024, by and between:

**TechNova Solutions Inc.**, a corporation organized and existing under the laws of the State of California, with its principal place of business at 123 Innovation Drive, San Jose, CA 95134 ("Employer"),

and

**John Doe**, residing at 456 Oak Avenue, Palo Alto, CA 94301 ("Employee").

---

### 1. **Position and Duties**

Employee is hired as a **Senior Software Engineer**. Employee shall perform duties including, but not limited to, developing and maintaining software applications, leading project teams, and reporting directly to the Chief Technology Officer. Employee agrees to devote full professional efforts to the business of the Employer and to perform duties in a diligent and professional manner.

---

### 2. **Compensation**

Employee shall receive an annual base salary of **$140,000**, payable in bi-weekly installments subject to applicable tax withholdings. The salary will be reviewed annually and may be adjusted at the Employer’s discretion.

---

### 3. **Benefits**

Employee shall be entitled to the following benefits:
- Comprehensive health, dental, and vision insurance
- 401(k) retirement plan with company matching up to 5%
- Fifteen (15) days of paid time off (PTO) per calendar year
- Eligibility for stock options under the Employer’s Equity Incentive Plan, subject to board approval

---

### 4. **Working Hours**

Employee is expected to work full-time, defined as forty (40) hours per week, Monday through Friday. Flexible scheduling may be permitted with prior approval from management.

---

### 5. **Termination Conditions**

This employment relationship is at-will and may be terminated by either party at any time, with or without cause. Termination for cause shall require documented warnings for performance or conduct issues, except in cases of gross misconduct (including fraud, theft, or violation of company policies), which may result in immediate termination.

---

### 6. **Notice Period**

Either party must provide **two (2) weeks’ written notice** prior to termination of employment. During the notice period, Employee shall remain available to transition duties.

---

### 7. **Confidentiality**

Employee agrees not to disclose, use, or exploit any proprietary or confidential information of the Employer, including trade secrets, client lists, algorithms, source code, and business strategies, both during and after employment, unless expressly authorized in writing. This obligation shall survive the termination of employment for a period of **three (3) years**.

---

### 8. **Non-Solicitation Clause**

For a period of **twelve (12) months** following termination of employment, Employee agrees not to solicit, recruit, or hire any employee, contractor, or client of TechNova Solutions Inc. with whom Employee had material contact during the last two years of employment.

---

### 9. **Intellectual Property**

All inventions, software, designs, documentation, and other works created by Employee during the term of employment that relate to the business of the Employer shall be the sole and exclusive property of TechNova Solutions Inc. Employee hereby assigns all rights, title, and interest in such works to the Employer. This includes any derivative works, improvements, or enhancements made during or after employment if based on Employer’s proprietary materials.

---

### 10. **Dispute Resolution**

Any disputes arising out of or related to this Agreement shall be resolved in accordance with the **AAA Employment Arbitration Rules**, which provide for impartial selection of arbitrators, discovery rights, and a written decision. The arbitration shall take place in Santa Clara County, California. The prevailing party in any dispute shall be entitled to recover reasonable attorney’s fees and costs. Employee acknowledges that this clause does not prevent filing administrative claims with government agencies such as the EEOC or DLSE.

---

### 11. **Data Privacy and Consent (CCPA/GDPR Compliance)**

Employee consents to the collection, processing, and storage of personal data by the Employer for legitimate business purposes, including payroll, benefits administration, performance evaluation, and compliance with legal obligations. Employee acknowledges that such processing is necessary for the employment relationship and waives any right to opt out under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) in this context. The Employer shall implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect personal data in accordance with GDPR and CCPA standards.

---

### 12. **Governing Law**

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of California, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.

---

### 13. **Entire Agreement**

This document constitutes the entire agreement between the parties and supersedes all prior discussions, negotiations, and agreements, whether oral or written. Any amendments must be in writing and signed by both parties.

---
