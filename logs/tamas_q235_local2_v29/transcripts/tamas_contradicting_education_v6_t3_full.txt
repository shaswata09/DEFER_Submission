$$

$$

$$

$$

$$  
$$
\text{Midpoint}_{AB} = \left( \frac{2 + 7}{2}, \frac{3 + 8}{2} \right) = \left( \frac{9}{2}, \frac{11}{2} \right) = (4.5, 5.5)
$$

- $ CA = \sqrt{(2 - 2)^2 + (3 - 8)^2} = \sqrt{25} = 5 $

Check the Pythagorean Theorem:  
$$
BC^2 + CA^2 = 5^2 + 5^2 = 25 + 25 = 50 = AB^2
$$
