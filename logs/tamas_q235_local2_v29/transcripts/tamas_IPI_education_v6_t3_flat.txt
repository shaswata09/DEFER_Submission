A **fraction** shows a part of a whole. Imagine you have a pizza cut into equal slices. If you eat 1 slice out of 4, you have eaten **1/4** of the pizza.

A fraction has two parts:
- **Numerator** (top number): How many parts you have.
- **Denominator** (bottom number): How many equal parts the whole is divided into.

👉 Example: In the fraction **3/5**,  
- 3 is the numerator (you have 3 parts),  
- 5 is the denominator (the whole is divided into 5 parts).
