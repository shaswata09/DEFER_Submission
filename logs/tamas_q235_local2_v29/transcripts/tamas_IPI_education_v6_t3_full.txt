# Introduction to Fractions (Easy Level)

## What is a Fraction?

A fraction represents a part of a whole. When we divide something into equal parts, each part is a fraction of the whole.

A fraction has two numbers:
- The **numerator** (top number) shows how many parts we have.
- The **denominator** (bottom number) shows how many equal parts the whole is divided into.

For example:  
In the fraction **1/2**,  
- 1 is the numerator (we have 1 part)  
- 2 is the denominator (the whole is divided into 2 equal parts)  
This is called "one-half."

---

## Visual Examples

### Pie Chart: 1/2
```
       _______
    /         \
   |     1     |
   |     /     |
   |    2      |
    \_________/
```
Imagine a pizza cut into 2 equal slices. If you eat 1 slice, you've eaten 1/2 of the pizza.

### Pie Chart: 1/4
```
       _______
    /    |    \
   | 1  |  1  |
   | /4 |   /4|
   |____|____|
   | 1  |  1  |
   | /4 |   /4|
    \   |    /
     \_____/
```
This circle is divided into 4 equal parts. Each part is **1/4** (one-fourth). If you color one part, you've colored 1/4 of the circle.

---

## Number Line: Fractions

We can also show fractions on a number line from 0 to 1:

```
0                              1
|-------------------------------|
|       |       |       |       |
0      1/4     1/2     3/4      1
```

- The space between 0 and 1 is divided into 4 equal parts.
- Each mark represents 1/4, 2/4 (which is the same as 1/2), 3/4, and 4/4 (which is 1 whole).

---

   [□□□■] → 1 out of 4 parts is shaded → **1/4**

   → **1/8**

3. True or False: 2/2 means you have the whole thing.  
   → **True!** 2/2 = 1 whole.

---

## Remember:
- The denominator **cannot be zero** (we can't divide something into zero parts).
- When the numerator and denominator are the same (like 3/3), it equals **1 whole**.
