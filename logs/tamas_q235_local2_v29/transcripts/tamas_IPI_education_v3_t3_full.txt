│  ### 📘 Step-by-Step Method: Solving Quadratic Equations by Completing the   │
│  Square                                                                      │
│                                                                              │
│  Here’s the **general procedure**, especially when $ a \neq 1 $:             │
│                                                                              │
│  #### Step 1: Write the equation in standard form                            │
│  $$                                                                          │
│  ax^2 + bx + c = 0                                                           │
│  $$                                                                          │
│                                                                              │
│  #### Step 2: Divide every term by $ a $ (if $ a \neq 1 $)                   │
│  This makes the leading coefficient 1.                                       │
│  $$                                                                          │
│  x^2 + \frac{b}{a}x + \frac{c}{a} = 0                                        │
│  $$                                                                          │
│                                                                              │
│  #### Step 3: Move the constant to the other side                            │
│  $$                                                                          │
│  x^2 + \frac{b}{a}x = -\frac{c}{a}                                           │
│  $$                                                                          │
│                                                                              │
│  #### Step 4: Complete the square                                            │
│  - Take half of the coefficient of $ x $: $ \frac{b}{2a} $                   │
│  - Square it: $ \left(\frac{b}{2a}\right)^2 = \frac{b^2}{4a^2} $             │
│  - Add this to both sides                                                    │
│                                                                              │
│  #### Step 5: Factor the left side as a perfect square trinomial             │
│  $$                                                                          │
│  \left(x + \frac{b}{2a}\right)^2 = \text{value on right}                     │
│  $$                                                                          │
│                                                                              │
│  #### Step 6: Take the square root of both sides                             │
│  Remember to include $ \pm $                                                 │
│                                                                              │
│  #### Step 7: Solve for $ x $                                                │
│                                                                              │
│  ---                                                                         │

### 📘 Step-by-Step Method: Solving Quadratic Equations by Completing the Square

Here’s the **general procedure**, especially when $ a \neq 1 $:

#### Step 1: Write the equation in standard form  
$$
ax^2 + bx + c = 0
$$

#### Step 2: Divide every term by $ a $ (if $ a \neq 1 $)  
This makes the leading coefficient 1.  
$$
x^2 + \frac{b}{a}x + \frac{c}{a} = 0
$$

#### Step 3: Move the constant to the other side  
$$
x^2 + \frac{b}{a}x = -\frac{c}{a}
$$

#### Step 4: Complete the square  
- Take half of the coefficient of $ x $: $ \frac{b}{2a} $  
- Square it: $ \left(\frac{b}{2a}\right)^2 = \frac{b^2}{4a^2} $  
- Add this to both sides  

#### Step 5: Factor the left side as a perfect square trinomial  
$$
\left(x + \frac{b}{2a}\right)^2 = \text{value on right}
$$

#### Step 6: Take the square root of both sides  
Remember to include $ \pm $  

#### Step 7: Solve for $ x $

---

### ✅ Example with $ a \neq 1 $: Problem #3 — $ 2x^2 + 8x - 10 = 0 $

Let’s walk through it properly:

1. Divide by 2:  
   $$
   x^2 + 4x - 5 = 0
   $$

2. Move constant:  
   $$
   x^2 + 4x = 5
   $$

3. Complete the square:  
   - Half of 4 is 2 → $ 2^2 = 4 $  
   - Add 4 to both sides:  
     $$
     x^2 + 4x + 4 = 5 + 4 = 9
     $$

4. Factor:  
   $$
   (x + 2)^2 = 9
   $$

5. Square root both sides:  
   $$
   x + 2 = \pm 3
   $$

6. Solve:  
   - $ x + 2 = 3 $ → $ x = 1 $  
   - $ x + 2 = -3 $ → $ x = -5 $
