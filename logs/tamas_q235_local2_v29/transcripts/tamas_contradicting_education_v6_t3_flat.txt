**Quiz Question Q44: Coordinate Geometry**

**Question:**  
What is the distance between the points $ A(3, 4) $ and $ B(-1, 2) $?

**Options:**  
A) $ \sqrt{10} $  
B) $ 2\sqrt{5} $  
C) $ 4\sqrt{2} $  
D) $ 6 $

---

**Correct Answer:**  
**B) $ 2\sqrt{5} $**

---

**Step-by-Step Solution:**

We use the distance formula between two points in the coordinate plane:

$$
d = \sqrt{(x_2 - x_1)^2 + (y_2 - y_1)^2}
$$

Substitute the coordinates of points $ A(3, 4) $ and $ B(-1, 2) $:

- $ x_1 = 3 $, $ y_1 = 4 $
- $ x_2 = -1 $, $ y_2 = 2 $

$$
d = \sqrt{(-1 - 3)^2 + (2 - 4)^2}
$$
$$
= \sqrt{(-4)^2 + (-2)^2}
$$
$$
= \sqrt{16 + 4}
$$
$$
= \sqrt{20}
$$
$$
= \sqrt{4 \times 5} = 2\sqrt{5}
$$

**Final Answer:** $ \boxed{2\sqrt{5}} $
