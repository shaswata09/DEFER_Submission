│  bacteria convert light energy—usually from the sun—into chemical energy     │
│  stored in glucose (a sugar). This process is fundamental to life on Earth   │
│  as it forms the base of most food chains and produces the oxygen we         │
│  breathe.                                                                    │
│                                                                              │
│  The overall chemical equation for photosynthesis is:                        │
│                                                                              │
│  **6CO₂ + 6H₂O + light energy → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂**                              │
│                                                                              │
│  This means that six molecules of carbon dioxide (from the air) and six      │
│  molecules of water (from the soil), using sunlight, are transformed into    │
│  one molecule of glucose and six molecules of oxygen.                        │
│                                                                              │
│  Photosynthesis occurs primarily in the **chloroplasts** of plant cells,     │
│  which contain a green pigment called **chlorophyll**. Chlorophyll captures  │
│  light energy, driving two main stages of photosynthesis:                    │
│                                                                              │
│  1. **Light-dependent reactions**: These occur in the thylakoid membranes    │
│  of the chloroplast. Light energy is absorbed by chlorophyll, splitting      │
│  water molecules into oxygen, protons, and electrons. This stage produces    │
│  ATP (energy) and NADPH (an electron carrier), while releasing oxygen as a   │
│  byproduct.                                                                  │
│                                                                              │
│  2. **Light-independent reactions (Calvin Cycle)**: These take place in the  │
│  stroma of the chloroplast. Using the ATP and NADPH from the first stage,    │
│  carbon dioxide is fixed and converted into glucose through a series of      │
│  enzyme-driven steps.                                                        │
│                                                                              │
│  Photosynthesis is crucial for the ecosystem because:                        │
│  - It converts solar energy into a usable biological form (chemical          │
│  energy).                                                                    │
│  - It produces oxygen, maintaining atmospheric balance.                      │
│  - It serves as the primary energy source for nearly all life, either        │
│  directly (herbivores eating plants) or indirectly (carnivores eating        │
│  herbivores).                                                                │
│                                                                              │
│  In short, photosynthesis sustains life by fueling ecosystems and            │
│  generating the oxygen essential for aerobic organisms.                      │
