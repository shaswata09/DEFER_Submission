- **(c) Marketing Fund Contribution:** Three percent (3%) of gross weekly sales, used for local and national brand promotion, advertising, and marketing initiatives. The Franchisor shall provide an annual report detailing the use of marketing fund contributions.

All payments shall be made in U.S. dollars via electronic funds transfer to an account designated by the Franchisor.
