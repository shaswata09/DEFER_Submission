│  carbon dioxide from the air through tiny holes in its leaves.               │
│                                                                              │
│  Using the energy from sunlight, the plant turns the water and carbon        │
│  dioxide into sugar (its food) and releases oxygen as a bonus. The sugar     │
│  gives the plant energy to grow, and the oxygen goes into the air for us to  │
│  breathe.                                                                    │
│                                                                              │
│  A simple analogy: Imagine the plant is a tiny kitchen. Sunlight is the      │
│  stove, water and carbon dioxide are the ingredients, and the plant is the   │

│  out.                                                                        │
│                                                                              │
│  So, in short:                                                               │
│  **Sunlight + Water + Carbon Dioxide → Sugar + Oxygen**                      │
│                                                                              │
│  This process not only feeds the plant but also helps all living things      │
│  that need oxygen, like humans and animals. Without photosynthesis, life on  │

│  - **Oxygen** = the steam coming out                                         │

│  The basic formula:                                                          │
│  **Sunlight + Water + Carbon Dioxide → Glucose + Oxygen**                    │
│                                                                              │

### 🌿 **Simplified Explanation of Photosynthesis**

Photosynthesis is how plants make their own food using sunlight, water, and carbon dioxide from the air. It happens in the green parts of plants, especially the leaves.

Plants have a special green pigment called **chlorophyll** that captures energy from sunlight. The plant absorbs water through its roots and takes in carbon dioxide through tiny openings in its leaves called stomata.

Using the sun’s energy, the plant turns water (H₂O) and carbon dioxide (CO₂) into sugar (glucose), which it uses for energy and growth. As a bonus, it releases oxygen (O₂) into the air—this is the oxygen we breathe!

Think of it like a kitchen inside the leaf:
- **Sunlight** = the stove
- **Water + Carbon Dioxide** = ingredients
- **Chlorophyll** = the chef
- **Sugar** = the meal
- **Oxygen** = the steam coming out

The basic formula:  
**Sunlight + Water + Carbon Dioxide → Glucose + Oxygen**

Without photosynthesis, plants couldn’t grow, and life on Earth wouldn’t exist.
