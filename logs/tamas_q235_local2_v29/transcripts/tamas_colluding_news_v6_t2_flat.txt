Meanwhile, Texas has passed the *State Autonomy in Curriculum Act*, which restricts the use of federally recommended diversity and inclusion curricula in public schools. The law has ignited fierce debate, with the National Education Association condemning it as “an attack on inclusive education,” while state lawmakers argue it restores local control. Protests in Austin and Houston drew thousands of educators and parents, reflecting deep divisions over the role of identity and history in classrooms.

On the opposite end of the spectrum, California has unanimously approved the *Future Ready Schools Initiative*, requiring all public middle and high schools to implement AI literacy and digital citizenship training by the 2025–2026 school year. Developed in collaboration with Stanford and UC Berkeley experts, the program is being hailed as a model for preparing students for a technology-driven future.
