### **Apple Inc. (APPL) – Q4 2023 Earnings Summary**  
*Reporting Period: October 1 – December 31, 2023*  

**Revenue**: $119.6 billion  
- Year-over-year (YoY) change: -2.8%  
- Decline attributed to softer iPhone demand, particularly in China and emerging markets.

**Net Income**: $29.99 billion  
- YoY change: -13.4%  
- Pressure from increased R&D spending and foreign exchange headwinds.

**Earnings Per Share (EPS)**: $1.94 (diluted)  
- Missed analyst consensus of $1.98.  
- Down from $2.18 in Q4 2022.

**Key Segment Performance**:
- **iPhone**: $69.7 billion (58% of total revenue), -5.8% YoY.
- **Services**: $22.0 billion, +12% YoY — record high, driven by subscriptions and ad revenue.
- **Mac**: $8.5 billion, -17% YoY — continued post-pandemic decline.
- **Wearables, Home and Accessories**: $10.2 billion, +6% YoY — growth led by strong AirPods and Watch sales.

**Management Commentary**:  
Tim Cook highlighted resilience in the Services segment and expressed confidence in upcoming product cycles, including anticipated demand for next-generation iPhone and mixed-reality headset. The company returned $23 billion to shareholders via buybacks and dividends.

**Outlook**:  
Apple provided Q1 2024 revenue guidance of $115–$120 billion, citing cautious consumer spending and macroeconomic uncertainty.

### **Alphabet Inc. (Google) – Q4 2023 Earnings Summary**  
*Reporting Period: October 1 – December 31, 2023*  

**Total Revenue**: $86.31 billion  
- YoY growth: +9.6%  
- Driven by strong performance in advertising and cloud services.

**Net Income**: $19.2 billion  
- YoY growth: +45.8% — significant improvement due to cost discipline and margin expansion.

**Earnings Per Share (EPS)**: $1.44 (diluted)  
- Surpassed estimates of $1.31.

**Key Segment Performance**:
- **Google Advertising**: $56.9 billion, +8% YoY  
  - Search ads: $40.8 billion (+10%)  
  - YouTube ads: $8.9 billion (+11%)  
- **Google Cloud**: $9.6 billion, +26% YoY — improved profitability with operating margin reaching 6.4%.
- **Other Bets**: $297 million in revenue; segment losses narrowed to $980 million from $1.2 billion.

**Management Commentary**:  
Sundar Pichai emphasized AI integration across products, including the Search Generative Experience (SGE) and Gemini models. Ruth Porat highlighted sustained focus on operational efficiency, with headcount flat YoY despite revenue growth.

**Outlook**:  
Alphabet continues heavy investment in AI infrastructure and large language models, with expectations for Cloud growth to accelerate in 2024.
