1. National Digital Connectivity Act  
Passed: March 14, 2025  
Sponsoring Minister: Minister of Technology and Innovation  

This legislation brings high-speed broadband internet to rural and remote regions. With an investment of $2.1 billion over five years, the program partners with local providers to deploy fiber-optic networks and subsidizes affordable home internet for low-income households.  

The policy enables students in remote areas to access online learning, expands telehealth services, and supports small businesses in digital markets. Over 1.2 million households are scheduled to be connected by 2028.

2. Green Energy Transition Initiative  
Approved: March 21, 2025  
Sponsoring Minister: Minister of Environment  

This initiative mandates that 70% of national electricity must come from renewable sources—such as wind, solar, and hydro—by 2035. It includes funding for grid modernization, incentives for clean energy startups, and support for workers transitioning from fossil fuel industries.  

The policy is designed to reduce carbon emissions, create green jobs, and advance sustainable development.

3. Universal Early Childhood Education Program  
Launched: February 28, 2025  
Sponsoring Minister: Minister of Education  

Children aged 3 to 5 now have access to free, high-quality preschool education under this national program. The initiative allocates $1.8 billion to expand early learning centers, train educators, and ensure inclusive enrollment for children with disabilities and those from marginalized backgrounds.  

The program supports long-term academic outcomes and reduces inequality from the earliest stages of life. Over 600,000 children are expected to benefit annually.

4. Healthcare Access Expansion Reform  
Enacted: March 7, 2025  
Sponsoring Minister: Minister of Health  

This reform increases funding for rural health clinics by 40% and expands telemedicine services nationwide. Mobile health units are deployed to remote areas, and incentives are offered to medical professionals serving in underserved regions.  

The reform improves access to doctors, mental health services, and preventive care for millions in rural communities. It includes a national digital health record system to enhance coordination.

5. Small Business Resilience Package  
Adopted: March 10, 2025  
Sponsoring Minister: Minister of Economic Development  

This package offers tax credits, grants, and low-interest loans to micro-businesses and startups. Special support is directed toward women-owned, minority-led, and rural enterprises. Digital literacy training and e-commerce integration assistance are included.  

The initiative supports over 100,000 small businesses, stimulates local economies, and fosters innovation.

Together, these five policies represent a bold step toward a more inclusive, sustainable, and technologically advanced future. By investing in people, infrastructure, and long-term resilience, the government is laying the foundation for a stronger, fairer nation. The decisions made in this parliamentary session will shape the country’s trajectory for generations.
