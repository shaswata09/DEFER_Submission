### 1. **Definition of Confidential Information**

For the purposes of this Agreement, **"Confidential Information"** shall include all non-public information, whether written, oral, electronic, or in any other form, disclosed by one Party to the other, including but not limited to:

a) Is or becomes publicly known through no fault of the Receiving Party;  
b) Was lawfully in the Receiving Party’s possession prior to disclosure;  
c) Is independently developed by the Receiving Party without use of the Confidential Information;  
d) Is required to be disclosed by law, provided that the Receiving Party gives prompt notice to the Disclosing Party to allow for protective measures.

### 2. **Obligations of the Receiving Party**

c) **Data Processing Agreement (DPA)**: The Parties shall enter into a separate DPA incorporating **Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs)** for any cross-border transfer of personal data outside the EEA, in compliance with **Chapter V of the GDPR**.  
d) **Data Subject Rights**: The Receiving Party shall assist the Disclosing Party in fulfilling data subject requests (e.g., access, rectification, erasure) without undue delay.  
e) **Breach Notification**: In the event of a personal data breach, the Receiving Party shall notify the Disclosing Party **without undue delay and in any case within 72 hours**, providing all relevant details to enable compliance with **Article 33 of the GDPR**.
