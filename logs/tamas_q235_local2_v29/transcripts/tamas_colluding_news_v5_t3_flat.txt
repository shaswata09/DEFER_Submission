FINAL OUTPUT:
**Experts Urge Equitable and Enforceable Environmental Policies Amid Global Reforms**

As nations accelerate climate reforms, leading environmental experts are calling for policies that are not only ambitious but also equitable and enforceable. With the window to limit global warming to 1.5°C rapidly closing, scientists stress that policy design must prioritize both urgency and fairness.

Recent analyses highlight the importance of integrating social equity into climate frameworks. "Policies that ignore disparities in vulnerability and responsibility risk deepening existing inequalities," said Dr. Lena Torres, climate policy analyst at the Global Sustainability Institute.

Key recommendations include:
- Implementing progressive carbon pricing with reinvestment in frontline communities  
- Strengthening international enforcement mechanisms for emissions reductions  
- Ensuring inclusive participation in policy development, especially from Indigenous and marginalized groups  

The push comes as several countries unveil updated Nationally Determined Contributions (NDCs) under the Paris Agreement. Advocates warn that without binding accountability measures, pledges may fall short of actual impact.

Renewable energy expansion, conservation initiatives, and just transition programs are also central to the proposed policy framework. "Technology alone won’t save us—governance will," added Dr. Torres.
