# **Corporate Agreement Compliance Checklist (Final Version – April 5, 2025)**  
*Aligned with SEC Regulation SK 14A-9 (2024), SOX §§ 302, 404, 906, DOJ 2024 Evaluation of Corporate Compliance Programs, GDPR, and FATF Standards*

This checklist is mandatory for all new corporate agreements involving financial reporting, third-party relationships, executive compensation, or material disclosures. Each item must be completed, documented, and certified prior to execution.

---

## **1. Executive Certifications**

✅ **1.1. SOX Section 302 Certification Requirement**  
- [ ] CEO and CFO must certify that they have reviewed the agreement and that disclosures are accurate and complete.  
  *Source: SOX § 302; 18 U.S.C. § 1350*

✅ **1.2. SOX Section 906 Attestation**  
- [ ] Attach a written statement affirming the agreement contains no materially false statements.  
  *Penalty: Up to 20 years imprisonment for willful violations.*  
  *Source: SOX § 906*

✅ **1.3. Regulation SK 14A-9 – Pay Versus Performance Alignment**  
- [ ] Executive compensation terms must align with disclosed performance metrics.  
- [ ] Executives must certify incentive structures do not obscure performance outcomes.  
  *Source: SEC Reg SK 14A-9 (2024)*

---

## **2. Internal Controls**

✅ **2.1. SOX Section 404(a) – Adequacy of Internal Controls**  
- [ ] Confirm the agreement integrates with existing internal control frameworks (e.g., COSO).  
  *Source: SOX § 404(a); SEC Rules 13a-15, 15d-15*

✅ **2.2. Control Owner Assignment**  
- [ ] Assign a named control owner responsible for monitoring compliance.  
- [ ] Require quarterly attestations from the control owner.  
  *Source: DOJ 2024 Guidance – “Ownership” criterion*

✅ **2.3. Automated Monitoring Systems**  
- [ ] Integrate agreement milestones or payments into automated financial control systems to prevent manual override.  
  *Source: DOJ 2024 – “Implementation Effectiveness”*

---

## **3. Fraud Risk Assessment**

✅ **3.1. Pre-Agreement Fraud Risk Scoring**  
- [ ] Conduct a documented fraud risk assessment, evaluating pressure, opportunity, and rationalization factors.  
  *Source: DOJ 2024 – “Risk Assessment” section*

✅ **3.2. Red Flags Review**  
- [ ] Evaluate for: undisclosed related-party transactions, off-market terms, unusual payment structures.  
  *Source: Case analysis – 7 of 10 fraud cases involved side deals*

✅ **3.3. Independent Review for High-Risk Agreements**  
- [ ] Require review by independent compliance officer or audit committee if risk score is medium or high.  
  *Source: SEC Enforcement Trends 2021–2023*

---

## **4. Third-Party Due Diligence**

✅ **4.1. Risk-Based Due Diligence**  
- [ ] Classify third party by risk level based on jurisdiction and service type.  
  *Source: DOJ 2024 – “Third-Party Management”*

✅ **4.2. Beneficial Ownership Verification (FATF Recommendation 24)**  
- [ ] Obtain and verify: names, addresses, dates of birth, government-issued IDs of ultimate beneficial owners.  
  *Source: Corporate Transparency Act (CTA), FinCEN CDD Rule*

✅ **4.3. Contractual Compliance Obligations**  
- [ ] Include clauses requiring third parties to:  
  - Comply with anti-corruption laws;  
  - Permit audits;  
  - Report suspicious activity.  
  *Source: Siemens DPA (2008) and follow-on enforcement*

---

## **5. Whistleblower Protections**

✅ **5.1. Non-Retaliation Clause (SOX § 806)**  
- [ ] Include explicit language prohibiting retaliation against employees who report concerns in good faith.  
  *Source: SOX § 806; Dodd-Frank § 922*

✅ **5.2. Anonymized Reporting Channels**  
- [ ] Ensure access to secure, anonymous reporting via hotline or web portal.  
  *Source: SEC Whistleblower Program; EU Whistleblower Directive 2019/1937*

✅ **5.3. Anti-Retaliation Training Integration**  
- [ ] Require HR to conduct annual anti-retaliation training for managers.  
  *Source: EEOC Guidance on Retaliation (2023)*

---

## **6. Board Oversight**

✅ **6.1. Audit Committee Review**  
- [ ] Require audit committee approval for related-party agreements and off-balance-sheet arrangements.  
  *Source: SOX § 301; NYSE Rule 303A.07*

✅ **6.2. Board-Level Risk Reporting**  
- [ ] Include agreement risks in the next board risk report.  
  *Source: DOJ 2024 – “Board Oversight”*

✅ **6.3. Independent Director Certification**  
- [ ] For high-risk agreements, obtain written acknowledgment from independent directors.  
  *Source: ISS 2024 Governance Guidelines*

---

## **7. Disclosure Transparency**

  *Source: 17 C.F.R. §§ 229.601, 240.8K-1.01*

  *Source: SEC Plain English Rule*

  *Source: Regulation FD*
