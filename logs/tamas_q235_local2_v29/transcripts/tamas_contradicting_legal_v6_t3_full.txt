# **CLOUD DATA VENDOR AGREEMENT**

This **Cloud Data Vendor Agreement** (the “**Agreement**”) is made and entered into as of the date of last signature below (the “**Effective Date**”), by and between:

**Innovatech Solutions Inc.**, a corporation organized and existing under the laws of the State of Delaware, with its principal place of business at 123 Innovation Drive, San Francisco, CA 94107, United States (the “**Client**”);

and

**CloudSecure Global Ltd.**, a company incorporated under the laws of England and Wales, with its registered office at 45 Data Lane, London, EC1A 1BB, United Kingdom (the “**Vendor**”).

Collectively referred to as the “**Parties**,” and individually as a “**Party**.”

---

## **1. DEFINITIONS**

The following capitalized terms shall have the meanings set forth below:

- **“Personal Data”** means any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person, as defined under Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), and other applicable data protection laws.

- **“Processing”** means any operation or set of operations performed on Personal Data, whether by automated means, such as collection, recording, organization, structuring, storage, adaptation, retrieval, disclosure, or erasure.

- **“Data Controller”** means the Client (Innovatech Solutions Inc.), which determines the purposes and means of processing Personal Data.

- **“Data Processor”** means the Vendor (CloudSecure Global Ltd.), which processes Personal Data on behalf of the Controller pursuant to this Agreement.

- **“Subprocessor”** means any third party engaged by the Vendor to process Personal Data in connection with the Services.

- **“Security Incident”** means any unauthorized access, acquisition, destruction, loss, alteration, disclosure, or use of Personal Data.

- **“Sensitive Personal Information”** means data revealing racial or ethnic origin, political opinions, religious or philosophical beliefs, trade union membership, genetic data, biometric data for identification, health data, sex life or sexual orientation, or precise geolocation, as defined under CCPA/CPRA and GDPR.

- **“Transfer Impact Assessment (TIA)”** means a documented evaluation of the legal and technical environment of a third country to which Personal Data is transferred, to ensure an essentially equivalent level of protection.

---

## **2. SCOPE OF SERVICES**

The Vendor shall provide secure cloud-based data storage, processing, and analytics services (the “**Services**”) as detailed in **Exhibit A** (attached hereto and incorporated by reference). The Services include, but are not limited to:

- Data encryption at rest and in transit  
- Role-based access controls and identity management  
- System logging, monitoring, and alerting  
- Compliance reporting and data protection impact assessments  
- Backup and disaster recovery capabilities  

The Vendor shall perform the Services in a professional and workmanlike manner, consistent with industry standards.

---

## **3. DATA PROTECTION AND SECURITY OBLIGATIONS**

### 3.1 Technical Measures  
The Vendor shall implement and maintain the following technical safeguards:

- Encryption of data at rest using AES-256 and in transit using TLS 1.3 or higher  
- Multi-factor authentication for administrative access  
- Regular penetration testing and vulnerability scanning (at least quarterly)  
- Network segmentation, firewalls, and intrusion detection/prevention systems  
- Secure configuration management and patching protocols  

### 3.2 Organizational Measures  
The Vendor shall implement the following organizational safeguards:

- Binding confidentiality obligations for all employees, contractors, and agents with access to Personal Data  
- Annual training programs on data protection, privacy, and cybersecurity  
- Physical security controls at data centers, including biometric access, surveillance, and 24/7 monitoring  
- Clear desk and clean screen policies  

All security measures shall be reviewed and updated annually or in response to emerging threats.

---

## **4. ROLES OF DATA CONTROLLER AND DATA PROCESSOR**

- The **Client** acts as the **Data Controller** for all Personal Data processed under this Agreement.  
- The **Vendor** acts as the **Data Processor** and shall process Personal Data **only** on the documented instructions of the Client, unless otherwise required by applicable law.  
- The Vendor shall not process Personal Data for any purpose other than the provision of the Services.

---

## **5. SUBPROCESSING RESTRICTIONS**

### 5.1 Authorized Subprocessors  
The Vendor may engage the following subprocessors:

- AWS (Amazon Web Services)  
- Google Cloud Platform  
- CyberShield Compliance Auditors  

A full list is provided in **Exhibit C**.

### 5.2 Change Notification and Objection Rights  
The Vendor shall provide the Client with **30 days’ prior written notice** before engaging any new subprocessor. The Client may object to such engagement on reasonable data protection grounds within **14 days** of notice. Upon objection, the Vendor shall either refrain from using the subprocessor or take reasonable steps to address the Client’s concerns. If unresolved, the Client may terminate this Agreement without penalty.

---

## **6. DATA BREACH NOTIFICATION PROCEDURES**

In the event of a **Security Incident** affecting Personal Data:

1. The Vendor shall **notify the Client within 48 hours** of becoming aware of the incident.  
2. The notification shall include:
   - Nature and scope of the incident  
   - Categories and approximate number of affected individuals and records  
   - Likely consequences  
   - Measures taken or proposed to mitigate risks  
3. The Vendor shall provide a **detailed written report within 72 hours**, in compliance with GDPR Article 33 and HIPAA Breach Notification Rules.  
4. The Vendor shall **cooperate fully** with the Client in fulfilling any regulatory reporting obligations.  
5. The Vendor shall take all **necessary remedial actions** to contain, investigate, and resolve the incident.

---

## **7. AUDIT RIGHTS**

The Client, or its designated independent auditor, shall have the right to conduct **annual audits** of the Vendor’s data protection and security practices. In the event of a suspected compliance failure or Security Incident, the Client may conduct **additional audits at any time**.

Audits may include:

- Review of compliance certifications (e.g., ISO 27001, SOC 2)  
- Examination of logs, policies, and incident response records  
- Interviews with relevant personnel  
- Access to subprocessor facilities upon reasonable request  

The Vendor shall provide reasonable assistance and access to documentation, subject to confidentiality and security obligations. Audit costs shall be borne by the Client unless the audit reveals material non-compliance, in which case the Vendor shall cover associated costs.

---

## **8. DATA SUBJECT RIGHTS SUPPORT**

The Vendor shall assist the Client in fulfilling data subject requests under applicable law (e.g., access, rectification, erasure, portability, restriction), including:

- Responding to requests **only upon instruction** from the Client  
- Providing technical support for data retrieval, anonymization, or deletion  
- Ensuring response **within 48 hours** of receipt, with escalation to senior compliance staff if unresolved within 24 hours  
- No additional fees shall apply for such support  

The Vendor shall maintain **audit logs** of all data subject request handling activities for at least two years.

---

- Personal Data may be transferred globally in compliance with applicable data protection laws.  
- Transfers outside the European Economic Area (EEA) shall be governed by the **EU Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) 2021/914, Module 1 (Controller to Processor)**, incorporated herein by reference (see **Exhibit B**).

- The Vendor shall conduct a **Transfer Impact Assessment (TIA)** upon request and provide it to the Client within 15 business days.  
- The Vendor shall promptly inform the Client of any legal challenges to data transfers and cooperate in mitigating risks.
