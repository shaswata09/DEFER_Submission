**Escalating Conflicts Drive Global Humanitarian Crises in Middle East, Ukraine, and Sudan**

**Middle East: Israel-Gaza Conflict**  
Over 30,000 Palestinians have been killed in Gaza since October 7, 2023, according to the Gaza Health Ministry, with the figure verified by the United Nations. Displacement affects nearly 2 million people, and humanitarian access remains critically constrained. Israeli military operations continue amid failed ceasefire negotiations. Hamas-led attacks on October 7 resulted in over 1,400 Israeli deaths. The UN Security Council recently considered a ceasefire resolution, but it did not pass due to a veto.

**Ukraine-Russia War**  
Russian forces have intensified attacks in eastern Ukraine, particularly in Donetsk and Kharkiv. Incremental advances near Avdiivka and Chasiv Yar are reported by the Institute for the Study of War (ISW). Ukraine faces artillery shortages despite a recently approved $61 billion U.S. aid package. Civilian casualties exceed 10,000, with continued targeting of energy infrastructure.

**Sudan Civil War**  
The conflict between the Sudanese Armed Forces (SAF) and the Rapid Support Forces (RSF) has displaced over 10 million people—the largest displacement crisis globally. Fighting persists in Khartoum and Darfur, with rising reports of ethnic violence and sexual assault. The UN warns of imminent famine in North Darfur due to blocked aid routes.
