│                                                                              │
│  ### 2.1 Definition of Consent                                               │

│  specific, informed, and unambiguous indication of the data subject’s        │
│  wishes by which they, by a statement or by a clear affirmative action,      │

│  ### 2.2 Required Elements of Valid Consent                                  │
│  All consent mechanisms must include:                                        │

│  - No pre-ticked boxes or default permissions.                               │
