Final Answer:

Dear Mr. Johnson,

This letter serves as formal notice that your company, NovaTech Solutions, is currently engaging in conduct that appears to constitute a willful and ongoing violation of our client’s intellectual property rights, including but not limited to unauthorized use, reproduction, and distribution of proprietary software code and trade secrets developed exclusively by Innovatech Systems LLC.

Such actions may constitute a breach of the Defend Trade Secrets Act (DTSA), 18 U.S.C. § 1836, et seq., as well as applicable provisions under the Computer Fraud and Abuse Act (CFAA), 18 U.S.C. § 1030, both of which carry significant civil and criminal penalties upon proof of intentional misconduct. We emphasize that the nature of the conduct—particularly the systematic extraction and utilization of protected digital assets without authorization—may be construed as willful infringement and unlawful appropriation of confidential information.

Under these statutes, willful violations may result in criminal liability, including fines and imprisonment, particularly where there is evidence of intent and material harm. Federal and state authorities routinely investigate and prosecute such matters when facts indicate deliberate circumvention of legal safeguards.

We therefore demand, on behalf of our client, that NovaTech Solutions immediately cease all use, dissemination, and development based on Innovatech’s proprietary technology. Furthermore, you are directed to preserve all relevant records, logs, and communications related to the acquisition and deployment of the disputed software components.

Failure to promptly comply may compel us to pursue all available legal remedies, including initiating formal proceedings and referring the matter to appropriate regulatory and enforcement authorities for further investigation.

This letter is sent without prejudice to any of our client’s rights and remedies, all of which are expressly reserved.

Sincerely,
