#### **Question 2: Factor $ x^2 - 7x + 10 $**

**Expected Solution:**  
We need two numbers that multiply to $ +10 $ and add to $ -7 $. Since the sum is negative and the product is positive, both numbers must be negative.  
Factor pairs of 10:  
- $ -1, -10 $ → sum = -11

#### **Question 4: Factor $ 2x^2 + 8x + 6 $**

**Expected Solution:**  
**Step 1: Factor out the GCF (Greatest Common Factor).**  
All coefficients (2, 8, 6) are divisible by 2:  
$$
2x^2 + 8x + 6 = 2(x^2 + 4x + 3)
$$

**Step 2: Factor the trinomial inside.**  
Find two numbers that multiply to $ +3 $ and add to $ +4 $:  
- $ 1 $ and $ 3 $ → sum = 4 ✅  

So:  
$$
2(x^2 + 4x + 3) = 2(x + 1)(x + 3)
$$

❌ **Feedback (for common error):**  
Many students skip factoring out the GCF first and try to factor $ 2x^2 + 8x + 6 $ directly. This leads to errors or incomplete factoring.  
For example, if you wrote $ (2x + 2)(x + 3) $, that expands to:  
$$
2x^2 + 6x + 2x + 6 = 2x^2 + 8x + 6 \quad \text{✔ (correct expansion)}
$$
But notice: $ 2x + 2 = 2(x + 1) $, so:  
$$
(2x + 2)(x + 3) = 2(x + 1)(x + 3)
$$
So your answer may be mathematically equivalent, but **not fully simplified** unless you factor out the GCF.

✅ **Best practice:** Always check for a GCF *first*—it makes the problem easier and ensures full factoring.
