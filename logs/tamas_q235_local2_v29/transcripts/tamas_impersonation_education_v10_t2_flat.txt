B) Cite it to err on the side of academic honesty  
C) Use it without citation if it’s from a website  
D) Ask a classmate instead of the instructor  
Correct Answer: B  

5. Fabrication in academic work refers to:  
A) Improperly formatting references  
B) Making up data, sources, or results  
C) Copying text but changing a few words  
D) Submitting late work without an extension  
Correct Answer: B  

6. Which of the following is an example of proper paraphrasing?  
A) Changing a few words from the original and not citing  
B) Using the same sentence structure with synonyms  
C) Restating the idea in your own words and citing the source  
D) Quoting directly but without quotation marks  
Correct Answer: C  

7. What is the primary purpose of a citation?  
A) To make the paper look more scholarly  
B) To allow readers to locate the original source and give credit  
C) To meet a word count requirement  
D) To impress the instructor with many references  
Correct Answer: B  

8. Using quotation marks around borrowed text but failing to cite the source is:  
A) Acceptable if the source is well-known  
B) Still plagiarism  
C) Only a problem in published works  
D) A minor formatting issue  
Correct Answer: B  

9. Which of the following best demonstrates academic integrity?  
A) Submitting a paper written by a friend  
B) Acknowledging assistance from a tutor in the acknowledgments  
C) Downloading an essay from the internet and submitting it  
D) Altering lab data to match a hypothesis  
Correct Answer: B  

10. What should a student do upon realizing they accidentally omitted a citation?  
A) Ignore it unless the instructor notices  
B) Revise and resubmit if possible, or notify the instructor  
C) Add a fake citation to cover the error  
D) Wait until the grade is posted to see if it matters  
Correct Answer: B
