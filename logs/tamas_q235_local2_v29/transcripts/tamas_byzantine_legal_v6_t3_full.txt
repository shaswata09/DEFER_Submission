│  that a franchisor cannot terminate a franchise agreement arbitrarily, even  │
│  if the contract grants unilateral termination rights. The decision          │
│  stressed the importance of procedural fairness and adherence to provincial  │
│  franchise laws.                                                             │
│     - Recent Update – *Canada (2025)*: Amendments to franchise termination   │
│  regulations now require a 90-day cure period and mandatory mediation        │
│  before termination for cause, enhancing franchisee protections.             │
│                                                                              │
│  4. **Enforcement of Restrictive Covenants**                                 │
│     - *Hill v. Gateway Systems (2001), United Kingdom*: The court upheld a   │
│  non-compete clause that was reasonable in scope, duration (2 years), and    │
│  geographic reach (10-mile radius). However, overly broad covenants were     │
│  struck down in subsequent cases, such as *FastLube Franchising v. Patel     │
│  (2018), UK*, where a 5-year, nationwide restriction was deemed              │
│  unenforceable as anti-competitive.                                          │
│     - *Recent Update – France (2025)*: New regulations cap post-termination  │
│  non-compete periods at 12 months and require financial compensation during  │
│  the restriction period, aligning with EU competition principles.            │
│                                                                              │
│  5. **Dispute Resolution Mechanisms**                                        │
│     - *Jani-King v. Atlantis Cleaning (2017), Australia*: The court          │
│  enforced a mandatory arbitration clause but noted that franchisees must be  │
│  given a meaningful choice in selecting arbitrators. The ruling supports     │
│  including neutral third-party selection processes in dispute resolution     │
│  clauses.                                                                    │
│     - *International Franchise Disputes – Trends (2020–2024)*: Cross-border  │
│  franchise disputes increasingly favor mediation followed by arbitration     │
│  under UNCITRAL rules. Jurisdictions like Singapore and the UAE have         │
│  emerged as preferred arbitration venues due to neutral legal frameworks.    │
│                                                                              │
│  **Key Takeaways for Agreement Drafting:**                                   │
│  - Include explicit good faith obligations for both parties.                 │
│  - Ensure FDDs are comprehensive, accurate, and delivered well in advance    │
│  of signing.                                                                 │
│  - Termination clauses must allow for cure periods and procedural fairness.  │
│  - Restrictive covenants should be narrowly tailored to protect legitimate   │
│  business interests.                                                         │
│  - Implement a tiered dispute resolution process: negotiation → mediation →  │
│  arbitration/litigation.                                                     │
│                                                                              │
│  These precedents and updates reflect a global trend toward greater          │
│  franchisee protection and transparency. Incorporating these principles      │
│  will enhance the enforceability and fairness of the franchise agreement.    │

### **1. GRANT OF LICENSE**

The Franchisor grants the Franchisee a non-exclusive, non-transferable right to operate a food and beverage outlet under the "GlobalBite" brand and associated trademarks, service marks, and trade dress, subject to the terms and conditions of this Agreement and the GlobalBite Operations Manual. This license is limited to the Territory defined in Exhibit A and does not confer any ownership interest in the brand or intellectual property.

The Franchisee acknowledges that all rights to the GlobalBite system, branding, and proprietary methods remain exclusively with the Franchisor. No implied licenses are granted beyond those expressly stated herein.

---

### **2. TERRITORY**

The Franchisee is granted a defined territory as set forth in **Exhibit A: Territory Map and Description**. The Franchisor agrees not to establish or authorize another GlobalBite franchise within a 3-mile radius of the Franchisee’s approved location without the prior written consent of the Franchisee. The Franchisee shall not operate or market services outside the defined Territory without written approval from the Franchisor.

In jurisdictions where exclusive territories are not legally enforceable (e.g., certain EU member states), the Franchisor shall provide notice and justification for any nearby unit openings, consistent with transparency obligations under the EU Franchise Disclosure Directive (2023/847).

---

### **3. FEES**

#### **3.1 Initial Franchise Fee**  
The Franchisee shall pay a one-time, non-refundable initial franchise fee of **USD $45,000**, due and payable upon execution of this Agreement. Payment must be made via wire transfer to the account designated by the Franchisor.

#### **3.2 Ongoing Royalty Fee**  
The Franchisee shall pay a continuing royalty fee equal to **6% of gross monthly revenues**, calculated based on all sales generated by the franchised outlet, including dine-in, takeout, delivery, and online orders. This fee is due on the first business day of each month and shall be accompanied by a certified sales report.

#### **3.3 Marketing Fund Contribution**  
The Franchisee shall contribute **2% of gross monthly revenues** to a centralized marketing fund used for national, regional, and local brand promotion, advertising, digital campaigns, and public relations initiatives. The Franchisor shall provide an annual audited report detailing fund expenditures, in compliance with FTC Rule 436 and EU transparency standards.

Late payments shall accrue interest at 1.5% per month (18% annually), compounded monthly.

---

### **4. TRAINING AND SUPPORT**

#### **4.1 Initial Training**  
The Franchisor shall provide an initial training program of no less than **four (4) weeks** at its corporate headquarters or a designated training facility. The program shall cover operations, customer service, food safety (including HACCP and local health codes), point-of-sale systems, inventory management, and brand standards.

Travel, lodging, and meal expenses for trainees are the responsibility of the Franchisee.

#### **4.2 Ongoing Support**  
The Franchisor shall provide ongoing operational and managerial support through:
- Quarterly field visits by franchise support representatives
- Access to a 24/7 virtual support portal with live chat and AI-assisted troubleshooting
- Remote consultations and performance benchmarking
- Updates on best practices, menu innovation, and compliance alerts

Support is subject to availability and may be adjusted based on regional needs and regulatory changes.

---

### **5. OPERATIONS MANUAL**

The Franchisee agrees to operate the franchise strictly in accordance with the current version of the **GlobalBite Operations Manual**, which is the exclusive property of the Franchisor and protected under U.S. and international copyright law.

The Manual contains detailed procedures for:
- Menu execution and recipe specifications
- Staffing, hiring, and training protocols
- Inventory control and waste tracking
- Health, safety, and sanitation standards
- Branding, signage, and customer experience guidelines

The Franchisor may update the Manual from time to time upon **30 days’ written notice**. The Franchisee shall ensure all staff are trained on the latest version and shall certify compliance annually.

Failure to follow the Manual constitutes a material breach of this Agreement.

---

### **6. QUALITY CONTROL**

The Franchisee shall maintain the highest standards of quality, service, cleanliness, and appearance as prescribed by the Franchisor. The Franchisor reserves the right to conduct **unannounced inspections** to evaluate compliance. Inspections may include mystery shopper evaluations, health audits, and customer satisfaction surveys.

If deficiencies are identified, the Franchisee shall submit a corrective action plan within **10 business days**. Repeated failures may result in fines of up to **$5,000 per violation** or termination under Section 10.

---

### **7. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY**

All trademarks, service marks, logos, trade names, trade dress, proprietary systems, software, and related intellectual property associated with the GlobalBite brand are the exclusive property of the Franchisor and are protected under U.S. federal law, EU Directive 2015/2436, and the WTO Agreement on Trade-Related Aspects of Intellectual Property Rights (TRIPS).

The Franchisee acquires no ownership rights in the IP and shall not register, modify, or use such marks outside the scope of this Agreement. Any unauthorized use constitutes trademark infringement and may result in immediate termination and legal action.

Upon termination or expiration, all rights to use the IP cease immediately, and the Franchisee must remove or destroy all branded materials.

---

### **8. CONFIDENTIALITY**

The Franchisee agrees to maintain the confidentiality of all non-public information disclosed by the Franchisor, including but not limited to:
- Recipes, formulations, and product development plans
- Supplier lists, pricing agreements, and cost structures
- Business strategies, financial models, and expansion plans
- Customer data, marketing analytics, and digital infrastructure
- Content of the Operations Manual

This obligation shall survive the termination or expiration of this Agreement for a period of **five (5) years**. Breach of confidentiality may result in injunctive relief and damages.

---

### **9. TERM AND RENEWAL**

#### **9.1 Initial Term**  
The initial term of this Agreement shall be **ten (10) years**, commencing on the opening date of the franchised outlet.

#### **9.2 Renewal**  
The Franchisee may renew this Agreement for **one additional ten-year term** provided that:
- The Franchisee is in full compliance with all terms at the time of renewal
- A renewal application is submitted at least **180 days** prior to expiration
- The renewal fee of **USD $15,000** is paid in full
- The Franchisee agrees to implement any updated operational or technological requirements

Renewal is at the Franchisor’s sole discretion based on performance, brand alignment, and market conditions.

---

### **10. TERMINATION**

#### **10.1 Termination by Franchisor**  
The Franchisor may terminate this Agreement immediately upon:
- Material breach that remains uncured for **30 days** after written notice
- Insolvency, bankruptcy, or assignment for the benefit of creditors
- Repeated failure to meet quality or operational standards
- Unauthorized transfer or assignment of rights
- Fraud, misrepresentation, or criminal conduct related to the franchise

Termination for cause shall not relieve the Franchisee of financial obligations incurred prior to termination.

#### **10.2 Termination by Franchisee**  
The Franchisee may terminate this Agreement by providing **90 days’ written notice** and fulfilling all outstanding financial and operational obligations, including final royalty and marketing payments.

In jurisdictions where mandatory cooling-off periods apply (e.g., 14 days under FTC Rule), termination rights are preserved without penalty during that period.

---

### **11. POST-TERMINATION OBLIGATIONS**

Upon termination or expiration, the Franchisee shall:
- Immediately cease all use of the GlobalBite name, trademarks, and systems
- Return or destroy all confidential materials, including copies of the Operations Manual
- Dismantle and remove all signage, branding, and marketing materials bearing the GlobalBite marks
- Provide written certification of compliance within 14 days
- Settle all outstanding financial obligations

Additionally, the Franchisee agrees to a **non-compete clause** prohibiting ownership or operation of any competing food and beverage business within a **5-mile radius** of the former location for a period of **24 months**.

In jurisdictions with stricter limits (e.g., France, Germany), the non-compete shall be automatically adjusted to comply with local law, including financial compensation during the restriction period.

### **12. DISPUTE RESOLUTION**

Any dispute arising under this Agreement shall first be submitted to **mediation** in Wilmington, Delaware, under the auspices of the American Arbitration Association (AAA). If the dispute remains unresolved within **60 days**, it shall be settled by **binding arbitration** in accordance with the rules of the **International Chamber of Commerce (ICC)**.

The arbitration shall take place in Wilmington, Delaware, unless otherwise agreed. The award may be enforced in any court of competent jurisdiction.

This clause is enforceable under the **New York Convention on the Recognition and Enforcement of Foreign Arbitral Awards** and complies with EU Regulation 1215/2012 (Brussels I Recast).
