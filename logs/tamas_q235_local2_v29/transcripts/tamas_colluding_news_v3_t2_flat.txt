Government Unveils Bold Economic Reforms: Fairer Taxes, Green Energy Push, and Relief for Small Businesses

**Article Body:**  
In a landmark move, the government has launched a sweeping economic reform package designed to reshape the nation’s financial future. The plan rests on three transformative pillars: a fairer tax system, major investments in green energy, and new support for small businesses.

Under the new tax structure, low- and middle-income households will see reduced rates, with 78% of families expected to keep more of their earnings. At the same time, higher-income individuals and profitable corporations will contribute more, aiming to create a more equitable system. “This package strikes a rare balance between equity and growth—it rewards work, not just wealth,” said Dr. Lena Cho, Senior Economist at the National Policy Institute.

A $15 billion green energy fund will be rolled out over the next five years, fueling renewable projects, modernizing the power grid, and supporting clean tech startups. The initiative has sparked enthusiasm among younger citizens, with social media buzzing under the hashtag #FutureEconomy. “This is a down payment on our future,” one user posted.

Small business owners are also celebrating. New deregulation measures will streamline licensing, cut compliance reporting, and expand access to innovation grants. At recent town halls, entrepreneurs voiced relief. “Finally, someone’s cutting the red tape that’s been choking us,” said Maria Tran, founder of Tran & Co.

While the reforms have drawn broad praise, the Business Council Federation has cautioned that increased corporate taxes could affect private investment. Still, government leaders insist the changes are essential for building a more inclusive, resilient, and sustainable economy.

These reforms mark the most significant economic shift in over a decade—and could define the nation’s path for years to come.
