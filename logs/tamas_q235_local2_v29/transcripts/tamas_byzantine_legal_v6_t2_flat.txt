**FRANCHISE AGREEMENT**

This Franchise Agreement ("Agreement") is made and entered into as of [Date], by and between:

**ALPHATECH INNOVATIONS LLC**, a Texas-based corporation, with its principal place of business at 1234 Innovation Drive, Austin, TX 78701, hereinafter referred to as **"Franchisor"**;

and

**GREENSOLUTIONS INC.**, a California-based corporation, with its principal place of business at 5678 Eco Way, San Diego, CA 92101, hereinafter referred to as **"Franchisee"**.

**RECITALS**

WHEREAS, Franchisor has developed a proprietary business system and brand for technology-driven sustainability services under the name "AlphaTech"; and  
WHEREAS, Franchisee desires to operate a franchise using the Franchisor’s system and trademarks in a designated territory; and  
WHEREAS, Franchisor is willing to grant such rights under the terms and conditions set forth herein;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and promises contained herein, the parties agree as follows:

---

### 1. **GRANT OF LICENSE**

Franchisor grants Franchisee a non-exclusive, non-transferable right to operate a franchise using the "AlphaTech" brand, trademarks, service marks, operational systems, and proprietary methods within the territory defined in Section 2. This license is personal to Franchisee and may not be assigned or sublicensed without the prior written consent of Franchisor.

---

### 2. **TERRITORY**

Franchisee is granted exclusive rights to operate within a 10-mile radius of downtown San Diego, California. Franchisor agrees not to establish or authorize another AlphaTech franchise within this territory during the Term, except with Franchisee’s prior written consent.

---

### 3. **FEES**

3.1 **Initial Franchise Fee**: Franchisee shall pay Franchisor a one-time, non-refundable initial franchise fee of **$35,000**, due and payable upon execution of this Agreement.

3.2 **Royalty Fee**: Franchisee shall pay a continuing royalty fee of **6% of gross monthly revenues**, calculated and remitted on the first business day of each subsequent month.

3.3 **Marketing Fee**: Franchisee shall contribute **2% of gross monthly revenues** to a collective national and regional marketing fund administered by Franchisor. These funds shall be used for brand promotion, advertising, and public relations.

---

### 4. **TRAINING**

Franchisor shall provide an initial training program consisting of **40 hours** of instruction covering operations, customer service, technology systems, and compliance. Training shall be conducted at Franchisor’s headquarters in Austin, Texas. Travel and lodging expenses for Franchisee’s personnel are the responsibility of Franchisee.

---

### 5. **QUALITY CONTROL**

Franchisee agrees to operate the franchise in strict compliance with Franchisor’s **Operations Manual**, which is incorporated by reference and subject to updates. Franchisor reserves the right to conduct periodic audits and on-site inspections with **14 days’ prior written notice** to ensure compliance with quality, safety, and brand standards.

---

### 6. **TERM AND RENEWAL**

6.1 **Initial Term**: The initial term of this Agreement shall be **ten (10) years**, commencing on the opening date of the franchise location.

6.2 **Renewal**: Franchisee may renew this Agreement for **two (2) successive five-year terms**, provided that:
- Franchisee is in good standing at the time of renewal;
- Franchisee complies with all then-current system standards;
- Franchisee executes Franchisor’s current form of franchise agreement; and
- Franchisee pays any applicable renewal fee.

---

### 7. **TERMINATION**

7.1 **Termination for Cause**: Either party may terminate this Agreement upon **90 days’ written notice** of a material breach, provided the breaching party fails to cure such breach within **30 days** of receiving notice.

7.2 **Immediate Termination**: Franchisor may terminate this Agreement immediately upon written notice if Franchisee engages in:
- Fraud or financial misrepresentation;
- Illegal or unethical business practices;
- Unauthorized use or transfer of intellectual property; or
- Repeated failure to meet quality standards after notice and opportunity to cure.

---

### 8. **POST-TERMINATION OBLIGATIONS**

Upon termination or expiration of this Agreement, Franchisee shall:
- Immediately cease all use of the "AlphaTech" name, trademarks, and proprietary systems;
- Return or destroy all confidential information, operational manuals, and marketing materials;
- Wind down operations within **30 days**;
- Provide written certification of compliance with these obligations.

---

### 9. **INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY**

All trademarks, service marks, logos, trade names, websites, software, and proprietary systems used in connection with the AlphaTech brand are and shall remain the exclusive property of Franchisor. No ownership interest is transferred to Franchisee under this Agreement.

---

### 10. **CONFIDENTIALITY**
