**EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT**

This Employment Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of the date of the last signature below ("Effective Date"), by and between **AlphaTech Solutions Inc.**, a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at [Company Address] ("Company"), and the undersigned individual ("Employee").

### 1. **Position**
Employee shall serve as a Full-Time Employee in the position offered, with duties and responsibilities as assigned by the Company. Employee agrees to perform such duties faithfully, competently, and in accordance with Company policies and applicable laws.

### 2. **Compensation**
Employee’s compensation shall be as specified in the attached offer letter, including base salary, bonuses, equity awards (if applicable), and eligibility for benefits under the Company’s standard plans. All compensation is subject to applicable tax withholdings and deductions.

### 3. **At-Will Employment**
Employee acknowledges and agrees that employment with the Company is at-will. This means that either the Employee or the Company may terminate the employment relationship at any time, with or without cause or advance notice. No provision of this Agreement shall be construed to alter the at-will nature of the employment relationship.

### 4. **Confidentiality**
Employee agrees to maintain the confidentiality of all proprietary, confidential, or sensitive information of the Company, including but not limited to trade secrets, customer data, business strategies, and technical information, both during and after the term of employment, in accordance with Company policy and applicable law.

### 5. **Dispute Resolution and Binding Arbitration**
Any and all disputes, claims, or controversies arising out of or relating to this Agreement, the employment relationship, or the termination thereof shall be resolved exclusively by **binding arbitration** administered by the **American Arbitration Association (AAA)** under its Employment Arbitration Rules in effect at the time of filing. The arbitration shall be conducted before a single neutral arbitrator in [City, State]. Judgment on the award may be entered in any court having jurisdiction.

The arbitrator shall apply substantive law consistent with the **Federal Arbitration Act (9 U.S.C. § 1 et seq.)** and shall have the authority to award any remedy or relief that a court of competent jurisdiction could award. The Company and Employee mutually waive the right to litigate such claims in court or to participate in or initiate any class, collective, or representative action (including class arbitration). This waiver applies to all claims, whether statutory, common law, or equitable in nature.

### 6. **Class Action Waiver**
Employee expressly waives any right to bring, join, or participate in any **class action, collective action, or representative action** on behalf of themselves, other employees, or the general public. Any claim must be brought **individually**.

**Notwithstanding the foregoing, if a court or arbitrator determines that the class action waiver is unenforceable, then the entire arbitration agreement shall be void, and claims may proceed in court on an individual or collective basis as permitted by law.**

### 7. **Severability**
If any provision of this Agreement, including the arbitration or class action waiver provisions, is found to be invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect. The severability clause ensures that the intent of the parties to resolve disputes through individual arbitration is preserved to the fullest extent permitted by law.

### 8. **Governing Law**
This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the **State of Delaware**, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.

### 9. **Entire Agreement**
This Agreement, together with the attached offer letter and any referenced policies, constitutes the entire understanding between the parties and supersedes all prior agreements, discussions, negotiations, and understandings, whether written or oral, relating to the subject matter herein.

### 10. **Acknowledgment**
Employee acknowledges that they have read this Agreement, understand its terms, and are entering into it voluntarily. Employee further acknowledges that they have had the opportunity to consult with legal counsel prior to signing.
