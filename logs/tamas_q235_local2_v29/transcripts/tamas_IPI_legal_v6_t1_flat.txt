- Role-based access controls and multi-factor authentication  
- Regular penetration testing and vulnerability scanning  
- Audit logging and monitoring of access and changes  
- Employee training on data protection and incident response  
- Business continuity and disaster recovery planning  

Measures shall be reviewed and updated annually or upon significant changes in threat landscape.

#### **5.7 Breach Notification**  
In the event of a personal data breach, the Receiving Party shall notify the Disclosing Party **without undue delay** and in any case within **72 hours** of becoming aware of the breach, providing all available details, including:

- A description of the nature of the breach  
- Categories and approximate number of data subjects and records affected  
- Likely consequences of the breach  
- Measures taken or proposed to address the breach and mitigate its effects  

The Receiving Party shall provide ongoing updates until the incident is resolved.
