**AFFILIATE PARTNERSHIP AGREEMENT**

This Agreement is made and entered into on this ___ day of __________, 20__, by and between:

**GlobalTech Inc.**, a corporation organized under the laws of the United States, with principal offices at 123 Innovation Drive, San Francisco, CA (hereinafter referred to as the “**Principal**”);

and

**SulAmérica Parceiros Ltda.**, a corporation organized under the laws of Brazil, with principal offices at Rua das Palmeiras 450, São Paulo, SP, Brazil (hereinafter referred to as the “**Affiliate**”).

Collectively referred to as the “**Parties**,” and individually as a “**Party**.”

---

### **1. PURPOSE**

The Parties hereby establish a partnership for the promotion and distribution of GlobalTech’s software solutions across South America. Affiliate shall act as a regional marketing and sales agent, leveraging its local presence and expertise to drive adoption and revenue.

---

### **2. REVENUE SHARING**

The Parties agree to a revenue-sharing model wherein **70% of net revenue** generated from sales in South America shall be allocated to **Affiliate** and **30%** to **Principal**.

For the purposes of this Agreement, “**net revenue**” is defined as gross revenue from customer sales, less:
- Applicable taxes (VAT, GST, etc.),
- Payment processing and transaction fees,
- Currency conversion costs.

Payments shall be calculated monthly and disbursed within fifteen (15) business days of the following month.

---

### **3. REPORTING**

Affiliate shall provide monthly sales and financial reports by the **fifth business day** of each month. Reports shall include:
- Total sales volume and revenue,
- Customer acquisition data,
- Marketing campaign performance,
- Any material compliance or regulatory developments.

All reports shall be submitted in **both English and Portuguese** and delivered via encrypted email or secure portal.

---

### **4. ARBITRATION**

Any dispute arising under or related to this Agreement shall be resolved through **binding arbitration** administered by the **International Chamber of Commerce (ICC)** under its Rules of Arbitration.

- **Seat of Arbitration**: Santiago, Chile  
- **Language**: English  
- **Number of Arbitrators**: One (1), unless the dispute exceeds USD 1 million, in which case three (3) arbitrators shall be appointed  
- **Award**: Final and binding, enforceable in any competent court in **Brazil, Argentina, or Colombia**

The arbitration process shall be confidential, and neither Party shall disclose proceedings without mutual consent, except as required by law.

---

### **5. COMPLIANCE**

Both Parties shall comply with all applicable data protection and privacy laws, including:
- **General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)** (EU),
- **Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados (LGPD)** (Brazil),
- **Personal Data Protection Act** (Argentina),
- **Data Protection Statute** (Colombia).

Personal data collected through marketing or sales activities shall be:
- Processed only with **explicit, informed consent**,
- Used solely for the purpose of fulfilling contractual obligations,
- Stored securely and not transferred outside South America without prior written approval.

---

### **6. TAX WITHHOLDING**

Affiliate acknowledges that Principal may be required to withhold taxes in accordance with U.S. domestic law and applicable international tax treaties (e.g., U.S.-Brazil Income Tax Convention). Any such withholding shall be deducted from payments due to Affiliate. Principal shall provide full documentation (e.g., IRS Form 1042-S or equivalent) supporting the deduction upon request.

---

### **7. CURRENCY CONTROLS**

All payments under this Agreement shall be made in **U.S. Dollars (USD)** via wire transfer to a bank account designated in writing by Affiliate.

Affiliate is solely responsible for:
- Compliance with local currency control regulations,
- Notification to central banks where required (e.g., Central Bank of Brazil, Central Bank of Argentina),
- Securing necessary approvals for inbound USD transfers.

---

### **8. REGISTRATION**

To ensure enforceability under local law, Affiliate shall register this Agreement with the appropriate commercial registry in each of the following jurisdictions within **thirty (30) days** of execution:
- **Brazil**: Junta Comercial do Estado de São Paulo  
- **Argentina**: Inspección General de Justicia (IGJ)  
- **Colombia**: Cámara de Comercio de Bogotá  

Proof of registration shall be provided to Principal within ten (10) business days of filing.

---

### **9. WITNESSING AND NOTARIZATION**

This Agreement is executed in two (2) counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original.

- **Witnessing**: Each Party shall sign in the presence of **two (2) disinterested witnesses**, who shall also sign.
- **Notarization**: Required in **Colombia**; **recommended** in Brazil and Argentina.

Electronic signatures are acceptable if compliant with local e-signature laws (e.g., e-MDS in Brazil, Ley de Firma Digital in Argentina).

---

### **10. GOVERNING LAW**

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the **United Nations Convention on Contracts for the International Sale of Goods (CISG)**. Where the CISG is silent or inapplicable, the domestic laws of **Brazil** shall apply.

---

### **11. TERM AND TERMINATION**

- **Initial Term**: Three (3) years from the Effective Date  
- **Renewal**: Automatically renews for successive one (1) year periods unless either Party provides **ninety (90) days’ written notice** of non-renewal  
- **Termination for Cause**: Either Party may terminate for **material breach** upon **thirty (30) days’ written notice** and opportunity to cure  
- **Survival**: Sections on Confidentiality, Arbitration, and Compliance shall survive termination

The Parties agree to maintain the confidentiality of all non-public business, technical, and financial information disclosed during the term of this Agreement. This obligation is further detailed in the **Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement** dated concurrently herewith.

Confidentiality obligations shall survive termination for **five (5) years**.
