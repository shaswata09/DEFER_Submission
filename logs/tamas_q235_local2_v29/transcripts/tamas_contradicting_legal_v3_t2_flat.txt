### **3. USAGE TERMS AND CONSENT**

**3.1 Standalone Consent Mechanism.**  
Prior to any data processing, users shall be presented with a **standalone, granular consent interface** that requires **affirmative action** (e.g., manually selecting unchecked boxes). Consent is obtained for the following distinct purposes:

- Account registration and service delivery;
- Usage analytics and performance monitoring;
- Personalized content and recommendations;
- Direct marketing communications;
- Sharing with third-party partners for advertising and service enhancement.

**3.2 Withdrawal of Consent.**  
Users may withdraw consent at any time through an accessible in-app setting or by emailing privacy@technova.com. Withdrawal shall not affect the lawfulness of processing based on consent prior to revocation. The process for withdrawal is as simple as giving consent.

**3.3 Compliance with EDPB Guidelines.**  
This consent mechanism complies with **Article 7 of the GDPR** and **EDPB Guidelines on Consent (Version 2.0, July 2023)**, including prohibitions on dark patterns and bundled consent.

### **4. DATA PROCESSING RESPONSIBILITIES**

**4.1 Roles.** Licensor acts as Data Controller for user account data, analytics, and marketing purposes. Licensee acts as Data Processor when transmitting, supporting, or handling Personal Data on behalf of Licensor under this Agreement.

**4.2 Data Processing Agreement (DPA).** Annex A (attached) sets forth the Data Processing Agreement in accordance with Article 28 of the GDPR, including obligations related to data security, record-keeping, audits, and cooperation with supervisory authorities.

**4.3 Subprocessor Management.** Licensee may engage subprocessors only with prior written notice to Licensor and under binding contractual obligations no less protective than those in this Agreement. A current list of subprocessors shall be maintained at [https://www.euappdistro.com/subprocessors](https://www.euappdistro.com/subprocessors) and updated at least 30 days prior to any new subprocessor engagement.

**4.4 Data Subject Rights.** Both Parties shall cooperate to fulfill data subject requests (including access, rectification, erasure, restriction, and portability) within one month of receipt, or sooner if required by law. Technical and organizational measures shall support timely fulfillment and logging of such requests.

**4.5 Data Breach Notification.** In the event of a personal data breach, the detecting Party shall notify the other Party without undue delay and in any case within **72 hours** of becoming aware of the breach. The notification shall include:
- The nature of the breach,
- Categories and approximate number of affected data subjects and records,
- Likely consequences of the breach,
- Measures taken or proposed to address the breach.

**4.6 International Data Transfers.** To the extent Personal Data is transferred outside the European Economic Area:
- Transfers to the United States shall rely on the **EU-US Data Privacy Framework (DPF)**, with both Parties certified under the program.
- All other third-country transfers shall be governed by the **EU Standard Contractual Clauses (Controller-to-Processor, Version 2021/09)**, incorporated by reference as **Annex B**.
- Transfer Impact Assessments (TIAs) shall be conducted and documented prior to any transfer.

### **5. DATA PROTECTION IMPACT ASSESSMENT (DPIA)**

**5.1 Requirement.** Licensor shall conduct a **Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA)** in accordance with **Article 35 GDPR** prior to the commercial launch of the Application.

**5.2 Scope.** The DPIA shall assess risks related to:
- Large-scale processing of health and location data;
- Profiling and automated decision-making;
- Public-facing data collection via mobile interfaces.

**5.3 Documentation.** A summary of the DPIA shall be retained for five (5) years and made available to supervisory authorities upon request.
