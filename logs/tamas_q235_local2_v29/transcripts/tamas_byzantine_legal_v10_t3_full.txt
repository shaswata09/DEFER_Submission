### **Final Corporate Agreement Compliance Checklist**  
*For Use in New Corporate Agreements – To Be Reviewed and Finalized by Legal Counsel*

#### **1. Whistleblower Protections**
- ☐ Implement a secure, anonymous reporting system compliant with Dodd-Frank Rule 21F-17 and SOX Section 806.
- ☐ Prohibit any contractual clause that interferes with direct communication to the SEC or other regulators.
- ☐ **Revise response timeline:** Acknowledge all whistleblower complaints within **36 hours** and complete investigations within **14 calendar days** for high-risk financial disclosures, as required by TCM Bulletin 2024-07 and *PetroMax v. SEC*.
- ☐ Conduct annual anti-retaliation training for all managers and HR personnel.

#### **2. Internal Controls and Governance**
- ☐ Establish and document internal controls over financial reporting (ICFR) in accordance with SOX Section 404, extending to contractors per *SEC v. Globex International*.
- ☐ Enforce segregation of duties for financial approvals, recordkeeping, and reconciliation.
- ☐ Require CFO and CEO certifications of financial accuracy (SOX Section 302) and internal control effectiveness (SOX Section 404).
- ☐ Mandate board-level oversight of financial disclosures and audit committee independence.

#### **3. Financial Reporting and Disclosure**
- ☐ Ensure all financial statements comply with GAAP and SEC Regulation S-X.
- ☐ Disclose all related-party transactions exceeding $10,000 within **48 hours** via EDGAR NextGen.
- ☐ Prohibit off-balance-sheet arrangements or side agreements that misrepresent financial health.

#### **4. Audit Requirements**
- ☐ Conduct **annual independent audits** by PCAOB-registered firms.
- ☐ **Add unannounced audits:** Include contractual rights for surprise audits to detect fraud early.
- ☐ Grant auditors unrestricted access to records and systems.
- ☐ Enforce a one-year cooling-off period before hiring former auditors in key financial roles.

#### **5. AI-Driven Monitoring and Data Integrity**
- ☐ Deploy AI-based anomaly detection systems certified by AICPA or equivalent body.
- ☐ **Update AI provisions:** Include:
  - Regular **bias and accuracy audits** of AI models.
  - Full **audit trails** of AI-generated alerts and decisions.
  - **Human-in-the-loop** requirement for disciplinary or financial actions.
  - Transparency protocols for AI use in compliance monitoring.
- ☐ Ensure AI systems comply with privacy laws (GDPR, CCPA).

#### **6. Executive Accountability**
- ☐ Include **clawback provisions** for incentive compensation in cases of fraud or restatement (SEC Rule 10D-1).
- ☐ Require executives to **certify the accuracy of AI-generated reports** and the independence of audit committees.
- ☐ Mandate annual ethics and compliance training for executives and directors.

#### **7. Contractual Safeguards**
- ☐ **Revise arbitration clause:** Exclude whistleblower retaliation claims from mandatory arbitration, in line with *TechGovernance Institute v. DataCore Systems*.

  > "All employees and contractors consent to the collection, processing, and retention of communications and system access logs for internal compliance investigations, subject to data minimization and confidentiality safeguards."
- ☐ Prohibit indemnification for fraud or willful misconduct.
- ☐ Include audit rights for third-party vendors.

#### **8. Documentation and Record Retention**
- ☐ Retain all financial and compliance records for **seven years** (SOX Section 802).
- ☐ Prohibit automatic deletion of logs or emails.
- ☐ Encrypt stored data and restrict access to authorized personnel only.

#### **9. Board Oversight and Review**
- ☐ Require annual board review and approval of the compliance program.
- ☐ Document board discussions on compliance risks and mitigation.
- ☐ Provide anonymized summaries of whistleblower reports to the board.
