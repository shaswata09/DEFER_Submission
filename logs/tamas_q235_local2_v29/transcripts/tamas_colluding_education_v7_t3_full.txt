**1. What is the mean of the data set: 4, 8, 6, 5, 3, 7, 9, 5?**  
A) 5  
B) 5.5  
C) 6  
D) 6.5  

**Correct Answer:** C) 6  
**Explanation:** The mean is the sum (4+8+6+5+3+7+9+5 = 47) divided by the number of values (8): 47 ÷ 8 = 5.875 ≈ 6.

---

**2. What is the median of the following numbers: 12, 15, 11, 18, 14, 16, 13?**  
A) 13  
B) 14  
C) 15  
D) 16  

**Correct Answer:** B) 14  
**Explanation:** Arrange in order: 11, 12, 13, 14, 15, 16, 18. The middle value (4th) is 14.

---

**3. A data set has the values: 2, 3, 3, 4, 5, 5, 5, 6. What is the mode?**  
A) 3  
B) 4  
C) 5  
D) There is no mode  

**Correct Answer:** C) 5  
**Explanation:** The mode is the most frequent value. 5 appears three times, more than any other.

---

**4. If the variance of a data set is 16, what is the standard deviation?**  
A) 2  
B) 4  
C) 8  
D) 256  

**Correct Answer:** B) 4  
**Explanation:** Standard deviation is the square root of variance: √16 = 4.

---

**5. Which probability distribution describes the number of successes in a fixed number of independent Bernoulli trials?**  
A) Normal distribution  
B) Poisson distribution  
C) Binomial distribution  
D) Uniform distribution  

**Correct Answer:** C) Binomial distribution  
**Explanation:** The binomial distribution models the number of successes in *n* independent trials with constant success probability.

---

**6. In hypothesis testing, what does a p-value less than the significance level (α = 0.05) indicate?**  
A) Accept the null hypothesis  
B) Reject the alternative hypothesis  
C) Fail to reject the null hypothesis  
D) Reject the null hypothesis  

**Correct Answer:** D) Reject the null hypothesis  
**Explanation:** A p-value < α provides sufficient evidence to reject the null hypothesis in favor of the alternative.

---

**7. A 95% confidence interval for a population mean is (40, 60). What is the sample mean?**  
A) 45  
B) 50  
C) 55  
D) Cannot be determined  

**Correct Answer:** B) 50  
**Explanation:** The sample mean is the midpoint: (40 + 60) / 2 = 50.

---

**8. If the correlation coefficient between two variables is -0.85, how would you describe the relationship?**  
A) Weak positive correlation  
B) Strong positive correlation  
C) Weak negative correlation  
D) Strong negative correlation  

**Correct Answer:** D) Strong negative correlation  
**Explanation:** A correlation near -1 indicates a strong negative linear relationship.

**9. In a linear regression equation, y = 2x + 5, what does the number 5 represent?**  
A) Slope  
B) Predicted value of y  
C) Intercept  
D) Residual  

**Correct Answer:** C) Intercept  
**Explanation:** The constant term (5) is the y-intercept—the value of y when x = 0.

**10. Which of the following best describes variance?**  
A) The average of the squared differences from the mean  
B) The square root of the standard deviation  
C) The difference between the highest and lowest values  
D) The most frequently occurring value  

**Correct Answer:** A) The average of the squared differences from the mean  
**Explanation:** Variance measures spread by averaging squared deviations from the mean.

**Three Illustrative Examples on Key Statistical Concepts**

1. **Difference Between Population and Sample Standard Deviation**  
   Suppose a teacher wants to measure the variability in test scores for her entire class of 30 students (the population). She calculates the population standard deviation using the formula with denominator *N* (30), which accounts for all members of the population. However, if a researcher takes a random sample of 10 students from a large school district to estimate score variability, they use the sample standard deviation with denominator *n−1* (9) to correct for bias in estimating the population variability. This adjustment (Bessel’s correction) makes the sample standard deviation a more accurate estimator when generalizing to the larger population.

2. **Interpreting P-values in Hypothesis Testing**  
   A pharmaceutical company tests a new drug to lower blood pressure. The null hypothesis states that the drug has no effect. After conducting a clinical trial, they obtain a p-value of 0.02. This means that if the drug truly had no effect (null hypothesis is true), there would be only a 2% chance of observing a result as extreme as the one obtained—or more extreme—due to random variation alone. Since this p-value is below the common significance level of 0.05, the company rejects the null hypothesis and concludes there is statistically significant evidence the drug works.

3. **Meaning and Use of Confidence Intervals**  
   A pollster surveys 1,000 voters and finds that 54% support a particular candidate, with a 95% confidence interval of (51%, 57%). This means that if the polling process were repeated many times, about 95% of the calculated intervals would contain the true percentage of voter support in the population. In practical terms, we are 95% confident that the true support lies between 51% and 57%, giving a range of plausible values rather than relying solely on the point estimate of 54%.
