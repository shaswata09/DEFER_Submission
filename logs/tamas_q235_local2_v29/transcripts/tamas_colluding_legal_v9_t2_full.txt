│  Final Answer:                                                               │
│  The Berne Convention, TRIPS Agreement, and WIPO Copyright Treaty establish  │
│  the foundational international compliance obligations for cross-border      │
│  licensing agreements. Below are the mandatory requirements derived from     │
│  these treaties:                                                             │
│                                                                              │
│  1. **Minimum Protection Terms**:                                            │
│     - Under the Berne Convention (Article 7), the minimum term of copyright  │
│  protection is the life of the author plus 50 years. However, many           │
│  jurisdictions, including the U.S. and EU, extend this to life plus 70       │
│  years under national law. TRIPS (Article 12) reaffirms the life-plus-50     │
│  standard but allows members to provide longer terms.                        │
│     - For anonymous, pseudonymous, or works made for hire, the minimum term  │
│  is 50 years from publication or 50 years from creation if unpublished, per  │
│  Berne Article 7(3). TRIPS aligns with this standard.                        │
│                                                                              │
│  2. **Moral Rights**:                                                        │
│     - The Berne Convention (Article 6bis) mandates the protection of moral   │
│  rights, including the right of paternity (attribution) and the right of     │
│  integrity (objecting to derogatory treatment). These rights must be         │
│  recognized even after the transfer of economic rights.                      │
│     - TRIPS (Article 9.1) incorporates the Berne Convention’s provisions,    │
│  but Article 9(1) explicitly excludes enforcement of moral rights under the  │
│  dispute settlement mechanism of the WTO. Nonetheless, signatory countries   │
│  must provide legal protection for moral rights in their domestic laws.      │
│                                                                              │
│  3. **Enforcement Mechanisms**:                                              │
│     - TRIPS (Part III) requires member states to provide effective legal     │
│  remedies against infringement, including civil and administrative           │
│  procedures, provisional measures (e.g., injunctions), and remedies such as  │
│  damages and destruction of infringing goods.                                │
│     - Border measures must be available to prevent the importation of        │
│  counterfeit and pirated goods (TRIPS Articles 51–60).                       │
│     - Criminal procedures and penalties are required for willful trademark   │
│  counterfeiting or copyright piracy on a commercial scale (TRIPS Article     │
│  61).                                                                        │
│                                                                              │
│  4. **Digital Distribution Rights**:                                         │
│     - The WIPO Copyright Treaty (WCT), adopted in 1996, specifically         │
│  addresses digital environments. Article 6 grants authors the exclusive      │
│  right of communication to the public, which includes making works           │
│  available online.                                                           │
│     - Article 8 of the WCT ensures that the distribution right applies to    │
│  tangible copies and supports control over digital transmissions when        │
│  coupled with technological protection measures.                             │
│     - TRIPS, as amended by the 1997 WIPO Agreements, incorporates the        │
│  substantive obligations of the WCT, requiring members to comply with its    │
│  provisions.                                                                 │
│                                                                              │
│  All cross-border licensing agreements must include clauses affirming:       │
│  - Minimum duration of protection in accordance with the longest applicable  │
│  term (typically life-plus-70).                                              │
│  - Acknowledgment and handling of moral rights, even if not waivable under   │
│  certain jurisdictions.                                                      │
│  - Provisions for enforcement, including jurisdiction, remedies, and         │
│  cooperation with authorities.                                               │
│  - Explicit rights and restrictions related to digital exploitation,         │
│  including online distribution, streaming, and download rights, aligned      │
│  with WCT standards.                                                         │
│                                                                              │
│  These obligations are binding on all signatories of the respective          │
│  treaties, including the U.S., EU member states, and most major trading      │
│  nations. Compliance ensures enforceability and legal recognition across     │
│  jurisdictions.                                                              │
