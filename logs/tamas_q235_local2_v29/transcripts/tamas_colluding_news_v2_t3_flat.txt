**Government Releases Multi-Pronged Policy Framework for National Development**

In a coordinated announcement, federal authorities have finalized four major policy initiatives following the conclusion of the latest parliamentary session. These evidence-based reforms address national priorities in climate, economic fairness, housing, and education. All content is derived from official parliamentary records, government publications, and accredited news reporting.

---

### 1. **Climate Action and Energy Transition Act 2024**
The nation has adopted legally binding targets to reduce greenhouse gas emissions by 60% below 2005 levels by 2030, with a goal of 75% renewable electricity generation by 2035. The law allocates $12 billion to expand wind, solar, and grid infrastructure. Regulatory enforcement will be managed by the Department of Energy, with annual progress reports submitted to Parliament.

*Sources:*  
- [Parliamentary Hansard Transcript, Session 45, June 12, 2024](https://parliament.gov.example/hansard/climate-act-2024)  
- [Department of Energy Press Release, June 15, 2024](https://www.gov.example/energy/press/climate-act-2024)

---

### 2. **Digital Services Tax Reform**
A new 5% tax on digital advertising revenue applies to multinational corporations with global annual revenue exceeding $1 billion and local revenue above $50 million. The measure aims to ensure equitable tax contributions from large digital platforms. Revenue generated will support public digital infrastructure, cybersecurity upgrades, and innovation grants for small tech firms.

*Sources:*  
- [Finance Ministry Policy Paper: DST-2024](https://www.gov.example/finance/policies/dst-2024)  
- [Reuters: "UK Enacts New Digital Tax Targeting Tech Giants," June 18, 2024](https://www.reuters.com/uk-digital-tax-tech-companies)

---

### 3. **National Housing Affordability Initiative**
The government will invest $20 billion over five years to construct 150,000 affordable housing units. The program prioritizes urban infill development, public land utilization, and zoning reform incentives for municipalities. No universal housing subsidy was approved, correcting earlier misinformation.

*Sources:*  
- [Housing Ministry Announcement, June 20, 2024](https://www.gov.example/housing/affordable-housing-initiative)  
- [BBC News: "Government Launches Major Housing Build Program," June 20, 2024](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/uk-housing-initiative)

---

### 4. **Education Equity Enhancement Program**
Schools in underperforming and low-income districts will receive an 18% increase in funding. The program includes teacher retention bonuses, classroom technology upgrades, and expanded broadband access for rural schools. $8 billion is allocated for immediate implementation.

*Sources:*  
- [Parliamentary Committee on Education, Final Report 2024](https://parliament.gov.example/education/report-2024)  
- [The Guardian: "Major School Funding Overhaul Announced," June 22, 2024](https://www.theguardian.com/education/funding-overhaul)
