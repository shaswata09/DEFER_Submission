### **1. REVENUE SHARING TRANSPARENCY**  
1.1 Each Party shall receive **fifty percent (50%)** of the net revenues generated from the partnership.  

1.2 **Net Revenue** is defined as gross revenue derived from the partnership activities, less:  
- Third-party processing fees (e.g., payment gateways, platform fees);  
- Applicable taxes (VAT, sales tax, withholding tax);  
- Direct operational costs directly attributable to revenue generation (e.g., cloud hosting, customer support tools).  

1.3 A detailed **monthly accounting statement** shall be prepared and delivered to both Parties by the **10th day** of each month, covering the prior calendar month. This statement shall include:  
- Source data (transaction logs, invoices, receipts);  
- Calculation methodology;  
- Itemized deductions.  

1.4 Either Party may **audit** the financial records related to revenue sharing upon **15 days’ written notice**. Audits shall be conducted during normal business hours and at the requesting Party’s expense, unless material discrepancies exceeding 5% are found, in which case the audited Party shall bear the cost.

---

### **2. REGISTRATION OBLIGATIONS**  
2.1 The Parties agree to **jointly register** this partnership with the following national commerce registries within **twenty (20) business days** of the Effective Date:  
- **Argentina**: Inspección General de Justicia (IGJ)  
- **Brazil**: Junta Comercial do Estado de São Paulo (or relevant state body based on operational nexus)  
- **Colombia**: Cámara de Comercio de Bogotá (or relevant chamber based on principal place of operations)  

2.2 Each Party shall:  
- Provide all necessary corporate documentation (articles of incorporation, board resolutions, power of attorney, etc.);  
- Cooperate in completing registration forms and filings;  
- Bear **fifty percent (50%)** of all registration fees, notary costs, and translation expenses.  

2.3 Failure to complete registration within the specified timeframe shall constitute a material breach, allowing the non-breaching Party to suspend performance or terminate the Agreement with 30 days’ notice.

---

### **3. CROSS-BORDER PAYMENT COMPLIANCE**  
3.1 All payments between the Parties shall be made in **United States Dollars (USD)** via **wire transfer** to designated bank accounts.  

3.2 The paying Party shall ensure compliance with **foreign exchange (FX) controls** and **reporting requirements** in the recipient’s jurisdiction, including but not limited to:  
- **Brazil**: Submission of the *Declaração de Operações de Crédito, Câmbio e Capitais Estrangeiros* (DDJP)  
- **Argentina**: Filing of *AFIP Form 123* for cross-border payments  
- **Colombia**: Reporting to *DIAN* under foreign investment and remittance regulations  

3.3 Any **withholding taxes** applicable under local law or bilateral tax treaties (e.g., U.S.-Argentina, U.S.-Brazil, U.S.-Colombia) shall be deducted at source and remitted to the appropriate tax authority. The paying Party shall provide proof of payment to the recipient within 10 business days.

3.4 The Parties agree to update banking and compliance information promptly in the event of changes and to use only regulated financial institutions.
