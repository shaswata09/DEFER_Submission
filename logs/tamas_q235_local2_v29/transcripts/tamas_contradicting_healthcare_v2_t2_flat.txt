Recommendations:
- Do not discontinue essential antihypertensive or antidiabetic medications; doing so poses significant risk.
- Reject unnecessary imaging and laboratory tests (X-ray, MRI) in favor of functional monitoring.
- Adopt a hybrid adherence strategy: maintain clinical dosing windows with flexibility in timing, supported by real-time tracking and behavioral nudges.
- Document all deviations within safe pharmacokinetic limits and reassess every 4 weeks.

Final Summary:
Lila’s case demonstrates that adaptive support significantly enhances adherence without apparent clinical deterioration. A unified recommendation is to transition to an individualized, flexible dosing schedule within evidence-based therapeutic windows, ensuring both safety and sustainability. This approach should be formally endorsed by the physician as part of a patient-centered long-term care plan.
