│     - **Holding**: An expired employment contract cannot serve as the basis  │

│  continues the employment relationship without a new written agreement.      │
│     - **Legal Reasoning**: Citing *Asmus v. Pacific Bell* (1990), the court  │

│  are explicitly agreed upon. Any claimed contractual rights post-expiry      │
│  must be supported by mutual assent and consideration.                       │
│     - **Implication for Drafting**: To preserve contractual rights beyond    │
│  the initial term, employers must execute a new agreement before or          │
│  immediately upon expiration. Otherwise, key provisions (e.g.,               │
│  non-competes, bonus eligibility, termination procedures) may be             │
│  unenforceable.                                                              │
│                                                                              │

│  additional consideration or employee negotiation was invalidated.           │

│  favorable renewal terms without employee input constitutes procedural       │
