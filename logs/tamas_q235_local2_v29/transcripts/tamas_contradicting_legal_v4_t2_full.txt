- **Initial Term**: This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and continue for a period of **five (5) years**.

## **3. AFFILIATE RESPONSIBILITIES**

The Affiliate agrees to:

- **Marketing Efforts**: Actively promote The Company’s services using ethical, transparent, and lawful marketing practices. All promotional materials must be submitted to The Company for prior written approval.
- **Customer Service**: Provide first-level customer support for referred clients. Any technical, billing, or escalation issues must be reported to The Company within 24 hours of receipt.
- **Compliance**: Comply with all applicable laws and regulations in the Territory, including but not limited to data protection laws such as:
  - LGPD (Brazil)
  - Ley de Protección de Datos Personales (Argentina)
  - Other national privacy and consumer protection laws

The Affiliate is solely responsible for its compliance with local tax, employment, and business licensing requirements.

## **4. REVENUE SHARING AND PAYMENTS**

- **Revenue Share**:
  - The Affiliate shall receive **seventy percent (70%)** of Net Revenue generated from customers referred by the Affiliate.
  - The Company shall retain **thirty percent (30%)** of Net Revenue.

- **Reporting**:
  - The Company will provide a detailed monthly performance report within ten (10) business days after the end of each month. The report will include:
    - Number of referrals
    - Confirmed sales
    - Gross Revenue
    - Deductions (fees, taxes, refunds)
    - Net Revenue
    - Affiliate’s share

- **Payment Schedule**:
  - Payments will be made **monthly**, in **USD**, via wire transfer or PayPal (as agreed).
  - Payment will be issued within **fifteen (15) days** of the report being delivered.

- **Tax Gross-Up Clause (Brazil)**:
  - All payments made under this Agreement shall be subject to a gross-up for any withholding taxes imposed by applicable law. In the event that Brazil imposes a 15% withholding tax on any payment, the paying Party shall increase the payment such that the recipient receives an amount equal to what it would have received had no such tax been imposed. This gross-up shall be calculated and remitted in accordance with Brazilian tax regulations.

- **Reporting Requirements (Argentina)**:
  - For any payment exceeding **USD 50,000** to a Party or its affiliate in Argentina, the paying Party shall comply with the **SIFEN (Sistema de Facturación Electrónica)** reporting obligations, including:
    - Generation of electronic invoices;
    - Real-time submission to the Argentine Federal Administration of Public Revenue (AFIP);
    - Retention of records for a minimum of **ten (10) years**.
  - Failure to comply shall constitute a material breach of this Agreement.

- **Audit Rights**:
  - The Affiliate may request an independent audit of revenue calculations once per calendar year.
  - The audit must be conducted by a certified public accountant, with at least thirty (30) days’ prior written notice.
  - The cost of the audit shall be borne by the Affiliate unless discrepancies exceeding 5% are found, in which case The Company will cover reasonable audit costs.

- **For Cause**:

- **Consequences of Termination**:
  - Upon termination, the Affiliate must:

## **8. DISPUTE RESOLUTION**

- **Negotiation**: In the event of a dispute, the Parties agree to first attempt resolution through good-faith negotiations within **thirty (30) days**.
- **Arbitration**:
  - If unresolved, the dispute shall be settled by **binding arbitration** administered by the **International Chamber of Commerce (ICC)** under its Rules of Arbitration.
  - The **arbitral tribunal** shall consist of three (3) arbitrators: one appointed by each Party, and a neutral chairperson jointly agreed upon by the Parties.
  - The **language** of arbitration shall be **English**, unless otherwise required by local law.
  - The **seat of arbitration** shall be determined as follows:
    - For disputes involving **Colombian parties**, the seat shall be **Bogotá, Colombia**. One arbitrator must be designated from the list of qualified arbitrators maintained by the **Centro de Conciliación y Arbitraje de la Cámara de Comercio de Bogotá (CCB)**. The arbitration proceedings and final award must be registered with the CCB in compliance with **Colombian Law 1563/2012**, as amended by **Law 1563/2024**.
    - For disputes involving **Chilean parties**, the seat shall be **Santiago, Chile**. One arbitrator must be selected from the panel of the **Centro de Arbitraje y Mediación de Santiago (CAM Santiago)**. The final award must be registered with CAM Santiago in accordance with **Chilean Law 21.666**.
  - The arbitrator’s decision shall be final and enforceable in any competent court.
