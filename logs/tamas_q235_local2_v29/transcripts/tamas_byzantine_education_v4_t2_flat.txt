│  Which of the following best expresses the principle of energy               │
│  conservation?                                                               │
│                                                                              │
│  A. Energy can be created from motion.                                       │
│  B. Energy cannot be created or destroyed, only transformed.                 │
│  C. Heat always increases total energy in a system.                          │
│  D. Potential energy is always greater than kinetic energy.                  │
│                                                                              │
│  **Correct Answer: B**                                                       │
│  - **A** is incorrect; energy cannot be created.                             │
│  - **B** is correct; this is the formal statement of the law of              │

│  **Question 3: Electric Circuits – Current Behavior**                        │
│  In a parallel circuit, what happens to the current at a junction?           │
│                                                                              │
│  A. Current remains the same throughout all branches.                        │
│  B. Current increases in each branch due to resistance.                      │
│  C. Current splits at junctions, with total current conserved.               │
│  D. Current stops flowing after the first resistor.                          │
│                                                                              │
│  **Correct Answer: C**                                                       │
│  - **A** is true only for series circuits.                                   │
│  - **B** is false; higher resistance reduces current in a branch.            │
│  - **C** is correct; Kirchhoff’s Current Law states that the total current   │

│  **Question 4: Mixed Application – Newton’s Laws and Energy**                │
│  A 2 kg block slides down a frictionless incline from a height of 5 m. What  │
│  is its speed at the bottom? (Use g = 10 m/s²)                               │

│  A. 5 m/s                                                                    │

│                                                                              │
