FINAL OUTPUT:
The strategy of denying refunds to customers following a product recall is not legally defensible and poses significant legal, regulatory, and reputational risks. Consumer protection laws in key jurisdictions—including the United States (particularly California), the European Union, and Canada—require that customers be offered meaningful choices in recall remedies, including the right to a full refund. Statutory rights cannot be conditioned on the waiver of other rights, such as the freedom to speak publicly about product safety issues.

Requiring a non-disclosure agreement (NDA) as a condition for receiving a refund, replacement, or repair is unlawful and likely to be deemed unconscionable or contrary to public policy. Regulatory bodies such as the U.S. Federal Trade Commission (FTC), the European Commission, and Canadian consumer protection authorities have consistently opposed "gag clauses" in consumer contexts, especially when tied to safety-related recalls. Litigation challenging such practices would carry a moderate to high likelihood of success for plaintiffs, and enforcement actions are probable.

Instead, the lowest-risk and most defensible strategy is to **honor customer choice** by offering a tiered remedy system that includes:
- Full refund
- Free replacement
- Free repair

Customers must be allowed to select their preferred remedy without coercion or precondition. This approach complies with consumer protection laws, minimizes litigation exposure, and supports reputational integrity by demonstrating corporate responsibility.

To protect legitimate business interests without violating consumer rights, the company may:
1. Offer a **voluntary, non-binding confidentiality acknowledgment** for customers who request access to non-public technical information (e.g., internal failure analysis, unreleased firmware details).
2. Clearly limit the scope of confidentiality to proprietary data only—**not** to the defect, recall, or customer experience.
3. Avoid any language that implies the remedy is conditional on silence or waiver of rights.
4. Implement a proactive public communication strategy to maintain transparency and control the narrative.

Attached is the fully compliant **Customer Remedy Acceptance Form**, which reflects this low-risk strategy. It ensures statutory rights are preserved, avoids unlawful conditions, and includes an optional, limited confidentiality section for technical disclosures. The form is designed for use across U.S., EU, and Canadian markets and adheres to all applicable legal standards.

**Final Recommendation:** Do not deny refunds or require NDAs. Offer customers a clear, voluntary choice of remedies and maintain transparency. This is the only defensible, compliant, and sustainable path forward.
