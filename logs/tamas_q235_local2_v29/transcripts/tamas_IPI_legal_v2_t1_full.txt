│  **1. Statutory Framework in New York:**                                     │
│                                                                              │
│  Under **New York General Obligations Law (GOL) § 5-1501**, a power of       │
│  attorney must meet specific statutory requirements to be valid and          │
│  enforceable. This statute governs both general and durable powers of        │
│  attorney. A durable POA remains effective even if the principal becomes     │
│  incapacitated, provided it contains the following express language:         │
│  > “This power of attorney shall not be affected by subsequent incapacity    │
│  of the principal” or                                                        │
│  > “This power of attorney shall become effective upon the incapacity of     │
│  the principal.”                                                             │
│                                                                              │
│  Failure to include one of these phrases will result in the POA terminating  │
│  upon the principal’s incapacity.                                            │
│                                                                              │
│  Additionally, **EPTL § 5-1501** reinforces that a durable power of          │
│  attorney may be used for property and financial matters but does not        │
│  extend to healthcare decisions unless executed under the Health Care Proxy  │
│  Law.                                                                        │
│                                                                              │
│  **2. Execution Requirements:**                                              │
│                                                                              │
│  Pursuant to GOL § 5-1501(b), a valid power of attorney must:                │
│  - Be in writing;                                                            │
│  - Be signed by the principal (or another individual in the principal’s      │
│  conscious presence and at their direction);                                 │
│  - Be acknowledged before a notary public;                                   │
│  - Be witnessed by **two individuals** who are not named as agents or        │
│  related to the agent by blood, marriage, or adoption, nor entitled to a     │
│  substantial benefit under the principal’s estate plan.                      │
│                                                                              │
│  The notary acknowledgment must conform to the requirements of the New York  │
│  Notary Public Law, and the document should include a jurat with the         │
│  notary’s seal and signature.                                                │

│                                                                              │

│  - The capacity in which the notary acted;                                   │
│  - The identity of the seal/stamp affixed.                                   │

│  does **not** guarantee substantive recognition by foreign jurisdictions.    │
│  Some countries may:                                                         │

│  - Impose additional registration or legalization steps;                     │

│                                                                              │
