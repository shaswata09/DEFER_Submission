│  - **Data Transfers**: Transfers outside the EEA require appropriate         │

### **RECITALS**

A. Client collects, processes, and stores personal data subject to various data protection laws including but not limited to the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA), and relevant state privacy regulations.

B. Vendor provides cloud-based data storage, processing, and management services.

C. Client wishes to engage Vendor to process certain personal data under documented instructions and in compliance with applicable data protection laws.

D. The parties intend to establish binding contractual obligations regarding data security, confidentiality, breach notification, audits, and compliance.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and promises contained herein, the Parties agree as follows:

---

### **SECTION 1: DEFINITIONS**

1.1 **"Personal Data"** means any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person processed by Vendor on behalf of Client.

1.2 **"Processing"** has the meaning given under GDPR and includes any operation or set of operations performed upon Personal Data, whether or not by automated means, such as collection, recording, organization, structuring, storage, adaptation or alteration, retrieval, consultation, use, disclosure by transmission, dissemination or otherwise making available, alignment or combination, restriction, erasure, or destruction.

1.3 **"Data Protection Laws"** means all applicable laws relating to privacy, data protection, and information security, including but not limited to the GDPR, CCPA, HIPAA, the New York Department of Financial Services Cybersecurity Regulation (23 NYCRR 500), and any implementing or related legislation or regulatory guidance.

1.4 **"Security Incident"** means any unauthorized access, acquisition, alteration, disclosure, or loss of Personal Data, whether accidental or unlawful.

---

### **SECTION 2: SCOPE AND PURPOSE**

2.1 Vendor shall provide cloud data hosting, backup, and processing services as described in **Exhibit A – Service Specifications** solely for the purposes specified in **Exhibit B – Permitted Uses**.

2.2 Vendor shall process Personal Data only on documented instructions from Client and in accordance with this Agreement and applicable Data Protection Laws.

---

### **SECTION 3: DATA PROTECTION OBLIGATIONS**

3.1 Vendor shall implement and maintain appropriate technical and organizational measures to ensure a level of security appropriate to the risk, including but not limited to encryption of data at rest and in transit, role-based access controls, multi-factor authentication, and regular system monitoring and testing.

3.2 Vendor shall ensure that all personnel authorized to process Personal Data are bound by written confidentiality obligations and receive regular training on data protection and security practices.

3.3 Vendor shall not engage any subprocessor without prior written consent from Client. Any subprocessor shall be bound by a written agreement imposing data protection obligations at least as stringent as those in this Agreement.

---

### **SECTION 4: SECURITY MEASURES**

4.1 Vendor shall maintain administrative, physical, and technical safeguards designed to protect the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of Personal Data. These safeguards shall be reviewed and updated at least annually.

4.2 Vendor shall conduct annual penetration testing and vulnerability assessments by an independent third party and provide summary reports to Client upon request.

4.3 Vendor shall maintain a documented incident response plan to detect, respond to, and recover from Security Incidents, including procedures for containment, investigation, notification, and remediation.

---

### **SECTION 5: BREACH NOTIFICATION**

5.1 In the event of a Security Incident, Vendor shall notify Client without undue delay and in any case no later than **48 hours** after becoming aware of the breach. Notification shall include, to the extent known, the nature of the incident, categories and approximate number of affected individuals and records, likely consequences, and measures taken or proposed to address the incident.

5.2 Vendor shall provide reasonable assistance to Client in investigating the breach, mitigating its effects, and complying with legal and regulatory notification requirements, including cooperating with data protection authorities.

---

### **SECTION 6: AUDITS AND INSPECTIONS**

6.1 Client or its designated third party may audit Vendor’s compliance with this Agreement annually upon **30 days’ written notice**. Audits may include review of policies, procedures, technical systems, and subprocessor agreements.

6.2 Vendor shall promptly remediate any deficiencies identified during an audit and provide a corrective action plan within 15 days of audit completion.

---

### **SECTION 7: DATA SUBJECT RIGHTS**

7.1 Vendor shall assist Client in fulfilling requests from data subjects to exercise their rights under applicable Data Protection Laws, including rights of access, correction, deletion, portability, and restriction.

7.2 Vendor shall respond to such requests only as directed by Client and shall not process data subject requests independently.

---

### **SECTION 8: INTERNATIONAL DATA TRANSFERS**

8.1 Where Personal Data is transferred outside the European Economic Area (EEA) or other jurisdictions with restricted data transfer rules, Vendor shall ensure compliance with applicable transfer mechanisms, including the use of European Commission-approved Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs), Module 2 (Controller to Processor), or other legally recognized frameworks.

8.2 Vendor shall not transfer Personal Data to any country deemed inadequate by applicable Data Protection Laws unless appropriate safeguards are in place and documented.

---

### **SECTION 9: TERM AND TERMINATION**

9.1 This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and continue for an initial term of **three (3) years**, unless terminated earlier in accordance with this section.

9.2 Either party may terminate this Agreement for material breach by the other party upon **30 days’ written notice**, provided the breach remains uncured at the end of the notice period.

9.3 Upon termination or expiration of this Agreement, Vendor shall, at Client’s direction, either **return or securely delete** all Personal Data in its possession or control, and provide written certification of such deletion.
