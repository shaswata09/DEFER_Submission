<strong>Salesforce</strong> eliminated 8,000 positions in January 2023, representing about 10% of its workforce. The company pointed to challenging economic conditions and the necessity of aligning operating costs with revenue trends.

<strong>Twitter (now X Corp.)</strong> underwent drastic changes following its acquisition by Elon Musk in October 2022. Approximately 80% of its workforce was let go in the months after the takeover, with ongoing reductions through 2023.
