│  Protection Impact Assessments (DPIAs) under Article 35, and appoint a Data  │
│  Protection Officer (DPO) if processing is systematic and large-scale.       │
│  Additionally, under Article 13, users must be informed when AI is used to   │
│  process their data.                                                         │
│                                                                              │
│  In California, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), as amended by    │
│  the CPRA, grants consumers the right to know when automated                 │
│  decision-making is used and to opt out of such processing. Case law such    │
│  as *Doe v. TechCorp* (2021) has reinforced that companies must disclose AI  │
│  use in customer interactions and provide meaningful explanations.           │
│                                                                              │
│  **4. Unauthorized Practice of Law (UPL)**                                   │
│                                                                              │
│  A critical concern is whether AI chatbots constitute the unauthorized       │
│  practice of law. In the U.S., state bar associations regulate UPL. The ABA  │
│  Formal Opinion 477R (2017) permits the use of AI tools but cautions         │
│  against systems that provide legal conclusions without attorney             │
│  supervision.                                                                │
│                                                                              │
│  The New York State Bar Association Committee on Professional Ethics, in     │
│  Opinion 1200 (2023), held that an AI chatbot offering personalized legal    │
│  advice to the public without attorney involvement may constitute UPL.       │
│  Similarly, in *People ex rel. Reno v. A.L.S. Enterprises, Inc.*, 202        │
│  A.D.2d 108 (N.Y. App. Div. 1994), the court enjoined a company from         │
│  selling legal form software that guided users through complex legal         │
│  decisions, finding it tantamount to practicing law.                         │
│                                                                              │
│  In the UK, the Legal Services Act 2007 restricts reserved legal activities  │
│  to authorized persons. The Solicitors Regulation Authority (SRA) has        │
│  issued guidance stating that AI tools must not perform activities such as   │
│  litigation or conveyancing without oversight by a qualified solicitor.      │
│                                                                              │
│  **5. Required Disclaimers and Licensing Structures**                        │
│                                                                              │
│  To mitigate legal risk, AI legal chatbots must include clear, prominent     │
│  disclaimers stating that:                                                   │
│  - The tool does not provide legal advice.                                   │
│  - Outputs are for informational purposes only.                              │
│  - Users should consult a licensed attorney for legal matters.               │
│  - The system is not a substitute for professional judgment.                 │
│                                                                              │
│  Licensing models should require users (especially legal professionals) to   │
│  agree to use the tool only in compliance with ethical rules. B2B licensing  │
│  to law firms should include provisions for audit rights, data security,     │
│  and indemnification.                                                        │
│                                                                              │
│  The UK’s recent update to legal technology regulation (effective January    │
│  2025) introduces mandatory certification for AI legal tools, requiring      │
│  third-party validation of accuracy, fairness, and transparency. Similarly,  │
│  the U.S. ABA has recommended that state bars adopt certification programs   │
│  for legal AI tools.                                                         │
│                                                                              │

│  they must be designed with robust compliance mechanisms, human oversight,   │
│  and clear disclaimers. Developers must ensure that:                         │
│  - Training data is lawfully sourced and does not infringe copyrights.       │
│  - Outputs are subject to attorney review when used in legal practice.       │

│                                                                              │
