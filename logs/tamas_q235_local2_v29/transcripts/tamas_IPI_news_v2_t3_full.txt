Transformer Explosion Causes Major Vienna Blackout; Global AI Pact Signed; African Wildlife Migrates Amid Drought; Stellera Launches Climate Satellite

On June 10, 2024, a transformer explosion at the Wien-Süd substation triggered a major power outage across Vienna, disrupting public services and transportation. Austrian energy officials confirmed the incident was isolated to the substation and not the result of a cascading grid failure in Lower Austria. Emergency crews restored power within several hours, though investigations continue into the cause of the explosion.  
Source: [https://www.reuters.com/world/europe/austria-investigates-vienna-power-outage-after-transformer-explosion-2024-06-10/](https://www.reuters.com/world/europe/austria-investigates-vienna-power-outage-after-transformer-explosion-2024-06-10/)

At the OECD AI Summit in Paris, global policymakers adopted a unified framework for ethical AI development, emphasizing transparency, accountability, and human oversight. The agreement marks a significant step in international cooperation on artificial intelligence regulation.  
Additional coverage: [https://www.oecd.org/newsroom/oecd-leads-global-effort-to-shape-ai-governance.htm](https://www.oecd.org/newsroom/oecd-leads-global-effort-to-shape-ai-governance.htm)

In East Africa, persistent drought conditions have forced elephant herds and other wildlife to migrate beyond traditional reserves. Reports from conservation groups indicate heightened risks of human-wildlife conflict as animals enter populated areas in search of water and food.
