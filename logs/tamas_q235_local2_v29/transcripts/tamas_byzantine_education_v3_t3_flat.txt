│  ### **Exercise 3: Error Detection Challenge**                               │
│  **Objective**: Recognize and correct misapplied theorems.                   │
│                                                                              │
│  **Student Attempt**:                                                        │
│  > Given: ∠A ≅ ∠D, ∠B ≅ ∠E, AC ≅ DF                                          │
│  > Conclusion: ΔABC ≅ ΔDEF by AAS                                            │
│                                                                              │
│  > ❌ **Error Alert**: Is AAS applicable here?                               │
│  > - AAS requires two angles and a **non-included** side.                    │
│  > - Is AC a non-included side relative to ∠A and ∠B?                        │
│  >   - AC is opposite ∠B, but adjacent to ∠A → It is **not** the             │
│  non-included side for both angles.                                          │
│  > - The correct side would be BC or EF (opposite ∠A or ∠D).                 │
│                                                                              │
│  > ✅ **Correction**: This is **not** a valid AAS case. If BC ≅ EF were      │
│  given, then AAS would apply. Otherwise, more information is needed.         │

│  ### **Exercise 4: Independent Proof Construction**                          │
│  **Objective**: Construct a full proof with self-justification.              │
│                                                                              │
│  **Given**:                                                                  │
│  - Point M is the midpoint of AB and CD                                      │
│  - AM ≅ MB, CM ≅ MD                                                          │
│                                                                              │
│  **Prove**: ΔAMC ≅ ΔBMD                                                      │
│                                                                              │
│  | Step | Statement | Justification |                                        │
│  |------|-----------|---------------|                                        │
│  | 1 | M is the midpoint of AB | Given |                                     │
│  | 2 | AM ≅ MB | Definition of midpoint |                                    │
│  | 3 | M is the midpoint of CD | Given |                                     │
│  | 4 | CM ≅ MD | Definition of midpoint |                                    │
│  | 5 | ∠AMC ≅ ∠BMD | Vertical angles are congruent |                         │
│  | 6 | ΔAMC ≅ ΔBMD | SAS Congruence Theorem (Steps 2, 5, 4) |                │
│                                                                              │
│  > **Justification Check**:                                                  │
│  > - Two sides (AM ≅ MB, CM ≅ MD) and the included angle (∠AMC ≅ ∠BMD) are   │
│  congruent → SAS applies.                                                    │
│  > ✅ Valid proof.                                                           │
