A Joint Venture Board (the "Board") shall govern strategic decisions. The Board shall consist of four (4) members:  
- Two (2) appointed by TechGlobal Inc.  
- Two (2) appointed by EuroSolutions GmbH.

All decisions of the Board shall require at least three (3) affirmative votes, including at least one vote from each Party’s appointees.

### **4.2 Day-to-Day Operations**

The Parties shall jointly appoint a Chief Operating Officer (COO) to manage daily operations. The initial term of appointment shall be two (2) years, subject to renewal by mutual written consent.

The COO shall report quarterly to the Board and shall have authority over staffing, procurement, and operational execution within approved budgets.

## **5. DECISION-MAKING**

The following major decisions require approval by the Board with at least three affirmative votes, including at least one from each Party:

- Approval of annual budgets exceeding USD 5,000,000;  
- Entry into new geographic markets outside North America and Europe;  
- Acquisition or disposal of assets valued at or above USD 1,000,000;  
- Hiring or termination of executive-level personnel (including COO);  
- Material changes to the core technology stack or product development roadmap.

Routine operational decisions may be made by the COO within approved parameters.

## **6. EXIT STRATEGIES**

### **6.1 Buy-Sell Mechanism**

After the third (3rd) anniversary of this Agreement, either Party may initiate a buy-sell process by submitting a written offer to acquire the other Party’s interest at a specified price. The recipient Party shall have thirty (30) days to either:
- Accept the offer and sell its interest; or  
- Elect to purchase the initiating Party’s interest at the same price.

The transaction shall close within 90 days of acceptance.

### **6.2 Drag-Along Rights**

If one Party sells its interest in the Joint Venture to a third party, it may require the other Party to join the sale under the same terms and conditions, provided the transaction is approved by the Board.

### **6.3 Tag-Along Rights**

If one Party receives a bona fide third-party offer to purchase its interest, the other Party shall have the right to participate in the sale on equivalent terms and conditions.
