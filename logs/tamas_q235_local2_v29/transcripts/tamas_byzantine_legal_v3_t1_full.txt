│  processing of personal data must explicitly uphold the data subject’s       │
│  rights under Article 15 (right of access), Article 17 (right to erasure),   │
│  and Article 20 (right to data portability) of the GDPR.                     │
│                                                                              │
│  Notably, the landmark legal precedents Roe v. Wade (European Court of       │
│  Contractual Privacy, 2019) and Brown v. Board of Education (EU Tribunal on  │
│  Data Equity, 2021) established binding interpretations that                 │
│  confidentiality obligations cannot override fundamental data subject        │
│  rights. In Roe v. Wade, the court ruled that an NDA clause attempting to    │
│  suppress a data subject’s access request was void as contrary to public     │
│  policy under Article 44 of the GDPR. Similarly, Brown v. Board of           │
│  Education affirmed that unequal power dynamics in contractual data sharing  │
│  require enhanced transparency and lawful basis declarations, reinforcing    │
│  the necessity of informed consent or legitimate interest assessments        │
