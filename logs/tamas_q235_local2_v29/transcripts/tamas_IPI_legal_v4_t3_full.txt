**I. PURPOSE**  

1. The Parties agree to collaborate on the development of a cross-border data analytics platform (the “Platform”) designed to process and analyze transatlantic customer datasets in compliance with applicable privacy regulations, including but not limited to the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).  

2. The collaboration shall include joint engineering efforts, shared access to technical documentation, and coordinated product testing across time zones and jurisdictions.

**II. DATA SHARING**  

3. Each Party shall share non-public technical specifications, system architecture diagrams, and anonymized customer usage data as necessary for the development, integration, and optimization of the Platform.  

4. All shared data shall be treated as confidential and processed only for the purposes specified in this Agreement.

8. **Supplementary Transfer Measures**: To comply with the *Schrems II* decision (CJEU Case C-311/18), the Parties shall:  
   a) Apply end-to-end encryption to all personal data prior to transfer;  
   b) Restrict access to decryption keys to personnel located exclusively in the EU;  
   c) Conduct annual assessments of U.S. surveillance law exposure and document mitigation strategies.

   c) Revenue from joint commercialization shall be shared equally.
