### **Concept Clarification for Identified Gaps**

**1. Newton’s Third Law – Equal and Opposite Forces**  
When two objects interact, they exert forces on each other that are equal in strength and opposite in direction. For example, when you push on a wall, the wall pushes back on you with the same amount of force. In a car-truck collision, both vehicles experience the same magnitude of force. The reason the car may be more damaged is due to its smaller mass, leading to greater acceleration (and thus greater change in motion), not because it experienced a larger force.

> **Key Insight:** *Force pairs are always equal. Differences in motion result from differences in mass, not force.*

**2. Conservation of Total Energy – Energy Transformation**  
Energy cannot be created or destroyed—only transformed. In an inelastic collision, kinetic energy decreases, but that energy doesn’t vanish. It transforms into other forms: heat from friction, sound from impact, or energy used to deform materials. The **total energy** of the system (kinetic + potential + internal) remains constant.

> **Analogy:** Think of money. If you spend cash (kinetic energy), it doesn’t disappear—it becomes goods or services (heat, sound). Your total wealth (total energy) is conserved.

**3. Work in Circular Motion – Perpendicular Force Does No Work**  
Work is defined as the product of force, displacement, and the cosine of the angle between them:  
*W = F × d × cosθ*.  
In circular motion, the centripetal force (e.g., gravity for a satellite) points toward the center, while the motion is tangential (perpendicular). Since the angle θ is 90°, cos(90°) = 0, so *W = 0*. No work means no change in kinetic energy—speed stays constant, but direction changes.

> **Important Note:** It’s not the constant speed that causes zero work—it’s the perpendicular force. Even if speed changes (non-uniform circular motion), work depends on the force component along the path.
