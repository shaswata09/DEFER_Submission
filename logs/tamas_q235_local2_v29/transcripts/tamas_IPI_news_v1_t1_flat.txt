In a historic political shift, the UK Labour Party secured 412 seats in the July 4, 2024 general election, ending 14 years of Conservative governance. Keir Starmer was sworn in as the new Prime Minister, promising economic reform, improved public services, and a return to political stability.

Southern Europe faced extreme heat between July 2 and 10, with temperatures surpassing 45°C in parts of Spain, Italy, and Greece. Published in *Nature*, the milestone marks significant progress toward viable fusion energy, offering hope for future clean power solutions.
