Employee’s employment with the Company is **at-will**. This means that either Employee or the Company may terminate the employment relationship at any time, with or without cause or notice. This at-will status cannot be altered except in a written agreement signed by an authorized officer of the Company.

### 4. **Binding Arbitration**
The Employee and the Company agree that any dispute, claim, or controversy arising out of or relating to this employment or the termination thereof—including but not limited to claims under federal, state, or local labor and employment laws, such as claims for discrimination, harassment, retaliation, wrongful termination, or wage and hour violations—shall be resolved **exclusively through final and binding arbitration**.

Such arbitration shall be administered by the **American Arbitration Association (AAA)** under its **Employment Arbitration Rules** in effect at the time a claim is filed. The arbitration shall be conducted before a single neutral arbitrator in **San Francisco, California**. The arbitrator shall issue a **written, reasoned award**, and judgment upon the award may be entered in any court of competent jurisdiction.

The arbitrator shall have the authority to award any remedies available under applicable law, including compensatory damages, injunctive relief, and attorneys’ fees where permitted by statute. However, **declaratory or injunctive relief shall be granted only on an individual basis** and not in a manner affecting other employees or the Company broadly.

### 5. **Class Action Waiver**
Employee **expressly waives** the right to bring, join, or participate in any **class action, collective action, private attorney general action, or representative action** against the Company, whether in court or in arbitration.

All claims must be brought **on an individual basis only**. This waiver applies to claims under the **Fair Labor Standards Act (FLSA)**, state wage and hour laws (e.g., California Labor Code), **Title VII of the Civil Rights Act**, the **Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA)**, the **Age Discrimination in Employment Act (ADEA)**, and any other federal, state, or local statute, regulation, or common law.

The arbitrator **shall have no authority** to:
- Consolidate or join claims of different individuals;
- Preside over any form of representative or class proceeding; or
- Issue relief on behalf of third parties not party to the arbitration.

This Agreement, including the arbitration and class action waiver provisions, shall be governed by and interpreted in accordance with the **Federal Arbitration Act (9 U.S.C. § 1 et seq.)**. To the extent state law applies, the laws of the **State of California** shall govern, without regard to conflict of laws principles.

If any provision of this Agreement is found to be invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect. **However, if the Class Action Waiver is found to be unenforceable**, then the entire arbitration provision shall be void, and any claims may proceed in a court of competent jurisdiction, but **only on an individual basis**.
