│                                                                              │

│  performance or compensation, does not fall into the category of documents   │
│  that require witnessing or notarization to be valid or enforceable. Its     │
│  legal effect stems from its content and the intent to notify the opposing   │
│  party of potential claims, not from formal execution requirements.          │
