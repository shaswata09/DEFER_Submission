### **2. DATA PROCESSING AND CONSENT**

The Parties agree that any processing of personal data shall comply fully with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR), Brazil’s Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados (LGPD) No. 13,709/2018, and Argentina’s Personal Data Protection Law No. 25,326, as amended.

**(a) Lawful Basis for Processing**  
Personal data shall only be processed where the data subject has provided unambiguous, informed, specific, and freely given consent, as required under:  
- GDPR Article 7: Consent must be verifiable, and the data controller must be able to demonstrate that consent was obtained.  
- LGPD Article 11: Consent must be prior, express, and informed, and may be revoked at any time.  
- Argentina Law 25.326, Article 5: Consent must be express (written or recorded) and revocable.

**(b) Transparency and Information**  
Data subjects shall be informed of:  
- The purpose of data processing;  
- The identity of the data controller;  
- The rights to access, rectify, delete, and object to processing;  
- The right to withdraw consent at any time;  
- Whether data will be transferred internationally, and the safeguards in place (e.g., adequacy decisions, SCCs).

**(c) Data Subject Rights**  
The Parties shall ensure mechanisms are in place to promptly respond to data subject requests, including access, correction, deletion, and portability, within the timeframes required by applicable law.

**(d) International Data Transfers**  
Cross-border transfers of personal data shall only occur:  
- To jurisdictions deemed adequate by the EU Commission, ANPD (Brazil), or Argentina’s Agencia de Acceso a la Información Pública; or  
- Where appropriate safeguards are in place, such as Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) approved by the relevant authority.

**(e) Data Protection Officer (DPO) and Record-Keeping**  
Where required, a DPO shall be appointed, and records of processing activities shall be maintained in accordance with GDPR Article 30 and LGPD Article 37.

**(f) Breach Notification**
