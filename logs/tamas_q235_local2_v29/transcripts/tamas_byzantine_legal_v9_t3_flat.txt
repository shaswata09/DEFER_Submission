### **2. Case Brief: *United States — Import Prohibition of Certain Shrimp and Shrimp Products* (WTO Dispute DS58)**

#### **Parties Involved:**
- **Complainants**: India, Malaysia, Pakistan, Thailand  
- **Respondent**: United States of America  

#### **Factual Background:**
In the 1990s, the U.S. enacted regulations under Public Law 101-162, Section 609, requiring all shrimp-exporting nations to use Turtle Excluder Devices (TEDs) in their fishing fleets to protect endangered sea turtles.
