│  ### **1. UNITED STATES – FEDERAL CORPORATE TAX LAW**                        │
│                                                                              │
│  #### **Legal Basis**                                                        │
│  The primary legal basis for federal corporate taxation is the **Internal    │
│  Revenue Code (IRC)**, specifically **Subchapter C (26 U.S.C. §§             │
│  301–385)**. Recent changes stem from the **Tax Fairness and                 │
│  Competitiveness Act of 2024**, enacted on December 15, 2024, and codified   │
│  under **Public Law 118-92**.                                                │
│                                                                              │
│  #### **Effective Dates**                                                    │
│  - **Effective Date:** January 1, 2025                                       │
│  - **Retroactive Application:** None                                         │
│  - **Transition Rules:** Corporations with fiscal years beginning between    │
│  July 1, 2024, and December 31, 2024, may elect to apply new rules           │
│  prospectively.                                                              │
│                                                                              │
│  #### **Key Provisions**                                                     │
│  - **Corporate Tax Rate Adjustment:** The federal corporate income tax rate  │
│  is reduced from **21% to 19%**, effective January 1, 2025.                  │
│  - **Global Intangible Low-Taxed Income (GILTI):** Reformed to apply a       │
│  **country-by-country basis**, with a minimum tax rate of **15%** for        │
│  foreign subsidiaries.                                                       │
│  - **Base Erosion and Anti-Abuse Tax (BEAT):** Modified to include digital   │
│  services and licensing payments, with a threshold increase from 3% to 5%    │
│  of deductible payments.                                                     │
│  - **Domestic Production Deduction:** Enhanced deduction of **12%** for      │
│  corporations engaged in advanced manufacturing.                             │
│  - **Reporting Requirements:** New **Form 9200 (Corporate Tax Transparency   │
│  Statement)** required for C-corporations with annual gross receipts         │
│  exceeding $50 million.                                                      │
│                                                                              │
│  #### **Potential Impacts**                                                  │
│  - Expected increase in corporate reinvestment due to lower tax burden.      │
│  - Multinational enterprises may restructure foreign holdings to comply      │
│  with GILTI reforms.                                                         │
│  - IRS enforcement activity anticipated to rise due to expanded reporting    │
│  obligations.                                                                │
│                                                                              │

│  ### **2. NEW YORK STATE CORPORATE TAX LAW**                                 │
│                                                                              │
│  #### **Legal Basis**                                                        │
│  Governed by the **New York State Tax Law, Article 9-A (Corporate Franchise  │
│  Tax)**. Amendments introduced via **Senate Bill S7512A**, signed into law   │
│  on November 10, 2024.                                                       │
│                                                                              │
│  #### **Effective Dates**                                                    │
│  - **Effective Date:** January 1, 2025                                       │
│  - **Filing Deadline Adjustments:** First filings under new rules due April  │
│  15, 2026 (for 2025 tax year).                                               │
│                                                                              │
│  #### **Key Provisions**                                                     │
│  - **Tax Rate Reduction:** The corporate franchise tax rate is reduced from  │
│  **6.5% to 5.75%** for businesses with New York receipts under $1 billion.   │
│  - **Water’s Edge Combined Reporting:** Now mandatory for all corporations   │
│  with in-state receipts exceeding $10 million.                               │
│  - **Digital Services Tax (DST):** Imposed at **5%** on revenue from         │
│  digital advertising, streaming, and data brokerage services.                │
│  - **Green Energy Credit:** Up to **$2 million** in tax credits for          │
│  corporations investing in renewable energy infrastructure.                  │
│  - **Apportionment Formula:** Shift from three-factor to **sales-only        │
│  factor** apportionment, aligning with MTC model.                            │
│                                                                              │
│  #### **Potential Impacts**                                                  │
│  - Encourages in-state investment and green technology adoption.             │
│  - Digital companies may shift operations or pricing strategies to mitigate  │
│  DST liability.                                                              │
│  - Simplified apportionment may reduce compliance disputes.                  │

│  ### **3. GERMANY – FEDERAL CORPORATE TAX LAW**                              │
│                                                                              │
│  #### **Legal Basis**                                                        │
│  Primary statutes include the **Körperschaftsteuergesetz (KStG –             │
│  Corporation Tax Act)** and **Gewerbesteuergesetz (GewStG – Trade Tax        │
│  Act)**. Recent changes enacted under the **2024 Economic Modernization      │
│  Ordinance (Wirtschaftsmodernisierungsverordnung 2024)**.                    │
│                                                                              │
│  #### **Effective Dates**                                                    │
│  - **Effective Date:** January 1, 2025                                       │
│  - **Phased Implementation:** Certain R&D incentives effective July 1,       │
│  2025.                                                                       │
│                                                                              │
│  #### **Key Provisions**                                                     │
│  - **Corporate Income Tax Rate:** Maintained at **15%**, with solidarity     │
│  surcharge (5.5% of tax) waived for SMEs with profits under €1 million.      │
│  - **Trade Tax (Gewerbesteuer):** Municipal rates remain, but a **national   │
│  cap of 14%** is introduced to limit regional disparities.                   │
│  - **R&D Super-Deduction:** 125% deduction for qualifying research           │
│  expenditures (increased from 100%).                                         │
│  - **Digital Tax Compliance:** Mandatory **E-Invoicing** and real-time       │
│  reporting via the **ZUGFeRD 3.0** system for all corporations.              │
│  - **Anti-Hybrid Rules:** Strengthened to align with EU Anti-Tax Avoidance   │
│  Directive (ATAD 3).                                                         │
│                                                                              │
│  #### **Potential Impacts**                                                  │
│  - Improved competitiveness for SMEs due to surcharge relief.                │
│  - Increased administrative burden from real-time reporting.                 │
│  - Incentivizes innovation through expanded R&D benefits.                    │
│                                                                              │

│                                                                              │

│  Administered under the **Income Tax Act (Cap. 134)**. Key amendments        │

│                                                                              │
│  #### **Effective Dates**                                                    │
│  - **Effective Date:** January 1, 2025                                       │
│  - **Staggered Compliance:** E-invoicing requirements effective January 1,   │
│  2026.                                                                       │
│                                                                              │
│  #### **Key Provisions**                                                     │
│  - **Corporate Tax Rate:** Remains at **17%**, but a **minimum effective     │
│  tax rate of 15%** applies to multinational enterprises (MNEs) with global   │
│  revenue > SGD 750 million, in compliance with OECD Pillar Two.              │
│  - **Pillar Two Implementation:** Qualified Domestic Minimum Top-up Tax      │
│  (QDMTT) introduced to protect local tax base.                               │
│  - **Green Investment Deduction:** 200% tax deduction for capital            │
│  investments in sustainable infrastructure.                                  │
│  - **Digital Platform Reporting:** Digital service providers must register   │
│  and report under the **Digital Services Tax Framework**.                    │
│  - **Transfer Pricing Documentation:** Enhanced requirements for Master      │
│  File and Local File submissions, aligned with BEPS Action 13.               │
│                                                                              │
│  #### **Potential Impacts**                                                  │
│  - Singapore remains competitive for MNEs despite minimum tax rules.         │
│  - Strong incentives for green finance and sustainable development.          │
│  - Increased compliance costs for digital platforms and large groups.        │
│                                                                              │

│  #### **(2) Effective Dates of Recent Changes**                              │
│  - **GILTI Reform (2025)**: Effective January 1, 2025, the U.S. implemented  │

│  - **BEAT Adjustments (2025)**: Amendments effective January 1, 2025,        │

│  - **Corporate Alternative Minimum Tax (CAMT)**: Enacted under the IRA,      │

│  corporations with average annual financial statement income exceeding $1    │
│  billion.                                                                    │
│                                                                              │
│  #### **(3) Key Provisions Introduced (Last 12 Months)**                     │
│  - **Pillar Two Alignment**: The U.S. Treasury issued final regulations (TD  │
│  9987, December 2024) adjusting GILTI to conform with the GloBE Model        │
│  Rules, including:                                                           │
│    - Introduction of a 15% minimum effective tax rate for foreign            │
│  subsidiaries.                                                               │
│    - Allowance for a substance-based income exclusion (up to 5% of payroll   │
│  and tangible asset costs).                                                  │
│  - **Enhanced Reporting Requirements**: New Form 8992 and Schedule G now     │
│  require granular reporting of income, taxes paid, and GloBE calculations    │
│  by jurisdiction.                                                            │
│  - **Green Incentives Expansion**: The IRS released guidance (Notice         │
│  2024-65) clarifying eligibility for 48C advanced manufacturing credits      │
│  under the IRA, with priority given to projects in energy communities.       │
│                                                                              │
│  #### **(4) Potential Impacts on Corporate Taxpayers**                       │
│  - Multinational enterprises (MNEs) may face increased compliance burdens    │
│  due to new reporting requirements.                                          │
│  - U.S. parented groups with operations in low-tax jurisdictions may see     │
│  higher effective tax rates due to GILTI adjustments.                        │
│  - Companies investing in clean energy may benefit from refundable credits,  │
