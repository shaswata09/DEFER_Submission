**COLLABORATION AGREEMENT**

This Collaboration Agreement (the “**Agreement**”) is made and entered into as of the 5th day of April, 2025 (the “**Effective Date**”), by and between:

NexaChain and CryptoVault may be referred to individually as a “**Party**” and collectively as the “**Parties**.”

---

### **RECITALS**

WHEREAS, the Parties desire to collaborate on the development and deployment of interoperable blockchain infrastructure for secure digital asset custody and cross-border settlement solutions;

WHEREAS, such collaboration involves the processing of personal data subject to the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), cross-border virtual asset transfers subject to the FATF Travel Rule, and operations in multiple jurisdictions including the EU, U.S., and Singapore;

WHEREAS, the Parties intend for this Agreement to comply with all applicable laws and regulations, including but not limited to the Markets in Crypto-Assets Regulation (MiCA), U.S. regulations issued by FinCEN, SEC, and CFTC, Singapore’s MAS Notices, GDPR, and OFAC sanctions requirements;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and promises contained herein, the Parties agree as follows:

---

### **1. PURPOSE OF AGREEMENT**

The Parties shall collaborate on the joint development, testing, and deployment of interoperable blockchain infrastructure enabling secure digital asset custody, transaction validation, and cross-border settlement services (the “**Project**”). No tokens, coins, or digital assets constituting securities under applicable law shall be issued, distributed, or created under this Agreement.

---

### **2. DATA PROTECTION AND GDPR COMPLIANCE**

**2.1 Data Transfer Mechanisms.**  
The Parties acknowledge that personal data may be transferred from the European Economic Area (EEA) to jurisdictions without an adequacy decision under the GDPR. To ensure compliance, the Parties hereby incorporate by reference:

- **Module Two (Controller to Processor)** and **Module Three (Processor to Processor)** of the Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) pursuant to the EU Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/914; and  
- **Annex A: Data Processing Addendum**, which forms an integral part of this Agreement.

**2.2 Data Processor Obligations.**  
Where one Party acts as a data processor on behalf of the other (the data controller), it shall:

(a) Process personal data only on documented instructions from the controller;  
(b) Ensure that all personnel authorized to process data are bound by confidentiality obligations;  
(c) Implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures (e.g., encryption, access controls, regular audits);  
(d) Permit the controller to conduct audits or inspections, with reasonable prior notice;  
(e) Engage sub-processors only with prior written consent and under equivalent data protection obligations;  
(f) Assist the controller in fulfilling data subject rights requests and in the event of a personal data breach, including notification obligations under Article 33 of the GDPR.

---

### **3. FATF TRAVEL RULE COMPLIANCE**

**3.1 Obligations.**  
Both Parties, in their capacity as Virtual Asset Service Providers (VASPs) or entities performing equivalent functions, shall implement and maintain compliance with **FATF Recommendation 16 (the “Travel Rule”)**. Each Party shall:

(a) Collect, verify, and securely transmit accurate originator and beneficiary information for all virtual asset transfers exceeding **USD/EUR 1,000**;  
(b) Ensure that such information includes at minimum: name, account number, and address or national ID for the originator, and name and account number for the beneficiary, in accordance with the IVMS 101 standard;  
(c) Protect the confidentiality and integrity of shared data using secure communication channels.

**3.2 Technical Integration.**  
The Parties shall integrate compatible Travel Rule compliance solutions (e.g., TRUST, Notabene, or similar) and ensure interoperability between their respective systems. Compliance shall be reviewed quarterly and updated to reflect evolving regulatory expectations under MiCA, FinCEN, and MAS frameworks.

---

The Parties shall negotiate in good faith to amend this Agreement to ensure compliance while preserving the commercial intent. If the force majeure event persists for more than **ninety (90) days**, either Party may terminate this Agreement by written notice.

---
