│  influence, ambiguity in scope, and non-compliance with formal execution     │
│  requirements. Below is a summary of key cases and legal principles that     │
│  highlight common vulnerabilities and judicial approaches:                   │
│                                                                              │
│  1. **In re Estate of Johnson, 2022 WL 1394852**                             │
│     - **Facts**: An 87-year-old testator with a diagnosis of moderate        │
│  dementia executed a POA naming her live-in caregiver as agent. Medical      │
│  records showed fluctuating cognitive function. The caregiver arranged for   │
│  the document’s preparation and notarization.                                │
│     - **Legal Challenges**: Lack of capacity and undue influence.            │
│     - **Court’s Holding**: The POA was invalidated. The court applied the    │
│  “substantial capacity” standard, requiring the testator to understand the   │
│  nature, extent, and consequences of the POA at the time of execution. The   │
│  lack of contemporaneous medical evaluation and the caregiver’s involvement  │
│  in the process created a presumption of undue influence, which was not      │
│  rebutted.                                                                   │
│     - **Key Takeaway**: Absence of medical corroboration of capacity on the  │
│  execution date and involvement of interested parties in the drafting        │
│  process significantly weaken the validity of a POA.                         │
│                                                                              │
│  2. **Estate of Thompson, 2020 Cal. App. Unpub. LEXIS 5421**                 │
│     - **Facts**: A POA granted the agent authority to “handle financial      │
│  matters” without further specification. The agent later transferred real    │
│  estate and large sums of money to themselves.                               │
│     - **Legal Challenge**: Ambiguity in the scope of authority.              │
│     - **Court’s Holding**: The transfers were voided. Under California       │
│  Probate Code § 4121, powers involving gifting, trust creation, or real      │
│  property transfers must be expressly stated. Vague language does not        │
│  confer such authority.                                                      │
│     - **Key Takeaway**: Broad or generic language fails to grant expansive   │
│  powers. Specificity is required for high-risk authorizations.               │
│                                                                              │
│  3. **Predicted Outcome in Similar California Cases**                        │
│     - In scenarios involving an elderly individual with cognitive            │
│  impairment, a POA executed under questionable circumstances (e.g.,          │
│  caregiver involvement, lack of independent witnesses) has a moderate        │
│  chance of being invalidated. California courts apply a heightened scrutiny  │
│  standard when fiduciary relationships or self-dealing are present.          │
│                                                                              │
│  **Common Loopholes and Weaknesses Identified**:                             │
