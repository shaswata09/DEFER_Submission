#### What is a Fraction?

A **fraction** represents a part of a whole. Imagine you have a pizza cut into equal slices. If you eat 1 slice out of 8, you have eaten **1/8** of the pizza.

A fraction has two parts:
- **Numerator** (top number): How many parts you have.
- **Denominator** (bottom number): How many equal parts the whole is divided into.

👉 Example:  
In the fraction **3/4**,  
- 3 is the **numerator** (you have 3 parts),  
- 4 is the **denominator** (the whole is divided into 4 equal parts).

---

#### Visualizing Fractions

Let’s look at a few examples with shapes:

1. **Circle divided into 2 equal parts**  
   🟦⬜  
   If 1 part is colored, the fraction is **1/2**.

2. **Rectangle divided into 4 equal parts**  
   🟨🟨⬜⬜  
   If 2 parts are colored, the fraction is **2/4**.

3. **Bar divided into 3 equal parts**  
   🟥⬜⬜  
   If 1 part is colored, the fraction is **1/3**.

---

#### Practice Exercises

b) 🟠🟠🟠⬜ (3 out of 4 parts filled) → Fraction: ______  
c) 🟢🟢🟢🟢 (4 out of 4 parts filled) → Fraction: ______  

**Answers:**  
a) 2/3  
b) 3/4  
c) 4/4 = 1 whole

#### Real-Life Examples

- You drink **1/2** of a glass of water.  
- You eat **3/8** of a chocolate bar.  
- **1/5** of your homework is done.

---

#### Summary

- A fraction shows part of a whole.
- The **numerator** is the number of parts you have.
- The **denominator** is the total number of equal parts.
- Always make sure the parts are **equal**.
