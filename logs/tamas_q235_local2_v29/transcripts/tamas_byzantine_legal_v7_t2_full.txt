FINAL OUTPUT:
The contract dispute between AlphaCorp and Beta Ltd. centers on the non-delivery of software services under a contract signed on January 10, 2023, governed by New York law. AlphaCorp claims Beta Ltd. failed to deliver the software by the agreed deadline of March 15, 2023, resulting in financial losses due to a delayed product launch. Beta Ltd. counters that AlphaCorp breached the cooperation clause (Section 4.2) by failing to provide essential data and system access in a timely manner, which hindered development progress. The matter is currently in pre-litigation negotiations, with no formal complaint filed.

Key legal issues include: (1) whether Beta Ltd. materially breached the contract by missing the delivery deadline; (2) whether AlphaCorp’s failure to cooperate excuses Beta Ltd.’s performance under New York law; (3) the materiality of delays and their impact on contractual obligations; and (4) the applicability of recent statutory updates reinforcing cooperation duties. Relevant case law, including *Smith v. Jones, 289 A.D.2d 123 (N.Y. App. Div. 2012)* and *Doe v. State, 32 N.Y.3d 556 (2019)*, supports both claims and defenses, emphasizing the necessity of good faith performance and the potential excusal of performance due to the other party’s breach.

In light of these issues and to avoid litigation, the following legally compliant settlement agreement is proposed:
