### 3. Recommended Additional Tests with Justification

Following clinical analysis, the following diagnostic tests are recommended:

- **Exercise Stress Test (Treadmill ECG):**  
  *Justification:* First-line test to evaluate for inducible ischemia. Will assess exercise capacity, ECG changes during exertion, and symptom reproduction. High yield given exertional symptom pattern and intermediate pretest probability.

- **Complete Blood Count (CBC):**  
  *Justification:* To rule out anemia as a contributing factor to exertional chest discomfort, particularly in patients with cardiovascular risk.

- **Chest X-ray:**  
  *Justification:* To evaluate cardiac size, pulmonary vasculature, and exclude structural lung disease or aortic pathology.

- **Optional: Coronary Artery Calcium (CAC) Scoring or CT Angiography:**  
  *Justification:* If stress test is inconclusive or intermediate risk, non-invasive imaging can quantify atherosclerotic burden.
