2. Which of the following constitutes proper citation in academic writing?

Correct Answer: B) Including an in-text citation and a full reference in the bibliography according to APA or MLA guidelines

3. You and a classmate discuss homework problems and help each other understand the concepts. Is this considered academic misconduct?

A) Yes, all collaboration is forbidden  
B) Only if you both submit identical answers without permission

D) Yes, because discussion leads to idea theft  

Correct Answer: C) No, as long as the collaboration is allowed by the instructor and you write your answers independently

4. Fabrication in academic work refers to:

A) Writing an essay using only secondary sources

Correct Answer: B) Creating false data, results, or sources and presenting them as real
