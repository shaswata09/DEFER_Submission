WHEREAS, Company A possesses advanced compliance algorithm technology and established client relationships in the United States;  

WHEREAS, Company B possesses software development infrastructure, EU regulatory expertise, and distribution channels in the European Union;  

WHEREAS, the Parties desire to form a joint venture for the purpose of developing, marketing, and distributing digital compliance software to financial institutions operating across the transatlantic market;  

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and promises herein contained, the Parties agree as follows:  

---

### **1. PURPOSE**  

The purpose of this Agreement is to establish a joint venture (the “JV”) for the development, marketing, and distribution of digital compliance software targeting transatlantic financial institutions.  

---

### **2. FORMATION**  

The Parties hereby form a joint venture under the name **“ComplyTransatlantic GmbH & Co. KG”**, governed by the terms of this Agreement. The JV shall be registered in Berlin, Germany, as a limited partnership (*Kommanditgesellschaft*). The general partner shall be a newly formed German limited liability company (GmbH), wholly owned by the Parties in proportion to their capital contributions.  

---

### **3. CONTRIBUTIONS**  

Each Party shall make the following contributions to the JV:  

#### **3.1 Company A**  
- USD 5,000,000 in capital;  
- Exclusive, royalty-free license to its proprietary compliance algorithm technology, subject to audit rights;  
- Access to its U.S. client network for marketing and sales purposes.  

#### **3.2 Company B**  
- EUR 4,200,000 in capital;  
- Software development infrastructure, including servers, development tools, and testing environments;  
- EU regulatory expertise and distribution channels, including relationships with EU financial institutions.  

All in-kind contributions shall be independently appraised prior to closing, and any valuation adjustments shall be reflected in the final capital accounts.  

---

### **4. GOVERNANCE**  

#### **4.1 Steering Committee**  
- The JV shall be governed by a Steering Committee composed of four (4) members: two (2) appointed by each Party.  
- All major decisions require unanimous approval, including:  
  - Annual budgets and financial plans;  
  - Hiring or termination of key personnel (including the Managing Director);  
  - Entry into contracts exceeding EUR 500,000;  
  - Changes to core business direction or product lines;  
  - Amendments to this Agreement.  

#### **4.2 Day-to-Day Management**  
- The Managing Director shall oversee daily operations. The initial Managing Director shall be appointed by Company B and serve a three-year term, subject to reappointment by the Steering Committee.  
- The Managing Director shall report quarterly to the Steering Committee and shall not take actions outside the approved budget or strategic plan without prior approval.  

---

### **5. PROFIT AND LOSS ALLOCATION**  

- Profits and losses of the JV shall be allocated annually in proportion to each Party’s capital contribution, adjusted for the appraised value of in-kind contributions.  
- Distributions shall be made semi-annually, net of reserves approved by the Steering Committee for operational continuity, debt service, or capital investment.  
- Financial statements shall be prepared in accordance with IFRS and audited annually by an independent firm jointly selected by the Parties.  

---

### **6. DISPUTE RESOLUTION**  

- Any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall be finally settled under the Rules of Arbitration of the International Chamber of Commerce (ICC) by three (3) arbitrators appointed in accordance with said rules.  
- The seat of arbitration shall be Geneva, Switzerland.  
- The language of the arbitration shall be English.  
- The arbitral award shall be final and binding and may be enforced in any competent court.  

---

### **7. GOVERNING LAW**  

- This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York, USA, for all operational, commercial, and contractual matters.  
- Matters concerning employment, labor relations, and local compliance within Germany shall be governed by the laws of the Federal Republic of Germany.  

---

### **8. DATA PROTECTION**  

- The Parties shall comply fully with:  
  - Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (General Data Protection Regulation, “GDPR”);  
  - 23 NYCRR 500 of the New York Department of Financial Services.  
- A Data Processing Agreement (DPA), attached as **Schedule A**, shall govern all data processing activities.  
- The DPA shall include Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) approved by the European Commission for data transfers from the EU to the U.S.  
- The JV shall appoint a Data Protection Officer (DPO) and conduct annual data protection audits.  

---

### **9. TRANSFER PRICING**  

- All transactions between the Parties and the JV, including licensing fees, service charges, and intercompany loans, shall comply with the arm’s length principle as defined under OECD Base Erosion and Profit Shifting (BEPS) Action 8-10.  
- Transfer pricing documentation shall be prepared annually in accordance with local requirements in both the U.S. and Germany and made available upon request by tax authorities.  
- The Parties shall cooperate in resolving any transfer pricing disputes.  

---

### **10. ANTITRUST COMPLIANCE**  

- The Parties affirm that this Agreement is not intended to, and shall not be construed to, restrict competition in violation of:  
  - Article 101 of the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union (TFEU);  
  - Section 1 of the Sherman Act (15 U.S.C. § 1).  
- The JV shall not engage in price-fixing, market allocation, bid-rigging, or any other anti-competitive conduct.  
- Annual antitrust compliance training shall be provided to all JV personnel.  

---

### **11. FOREIGN INVESTMENT DISCLAIMER**  

- Each Party acknowledges that this Agreement does not constitute an assignment of control over strategic assets subject to foreign investment review.  
- The Parties shall notify each other promptly of any proposed change in ownership or control that may trigger review under:  
  - The Committee on Foreign Investment in the United States (CFIUS);  
  - The German Foreign Trade and Payments Ordinance (Außenwirtschaftsverordnung, “AWV”).  
- Each Party shall bear its own costs related to such filings.  

---

### **12. TERM AND TERMINATION**  

#### **12.1 Term**  
- This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and continue for an initial term of **ten (10) years**.  
- It shall automatically renew for successive **five (5)-year terms** unless either Party provides written notice of non-renewal at least **twelve (12) months** prior to the end of the then-current term (the “Sunset Clause”).  

#### **12.2 Termination Events**  
Either Party may terminate this Agreement upon the occurrence of any of the following:  
- Material breach by the other Party, if uncured within sixty (60) days of written notice;  
- Insolvency, bankruptcy, or dissolution of the other Party;  
- Force majeure event preventing performance for more than 180 consecutive days.  

---

### **13. POST-TERMINATION**  

- Upon termination, the JV shall wind down in an orderly manner under the supervision of a liquidator jointly appointed by the Parties.  
- Assets shall be liquidated, and proceeds distributed in accordance with the profit-sharing ratios.  
- Intellectual property shall revert to the originating Party. However, any licenses necessary for ongoing customer support shall remain in effect for up to two (2) years, subject to fair compensation.  

---

### **14. MISCELLANEOUS**  

- **Entire Agreement**: This Agreement, including its schedules, constitutes the entire understanding between the Parties.  
- **Amendments**: Any amendment must be in writing and signed by both Parties.  
- **Notices**: All notices shall be in writing and sent to the designated addresses of each Party.  
- **Assignment**: Neither Party may assign its rights or obligations without the prior written consent of the other Party.  
- **Waiver**: Failure to enforce any provision shall not constitute a waiver.  
- **Severability**: If any provision is held invalid, the remainder shall remain in full force.  
- **Counterparts**: This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original.  
- **Language**: The Agreement is executed in English. In the event of any conflict, the English version shall control.
