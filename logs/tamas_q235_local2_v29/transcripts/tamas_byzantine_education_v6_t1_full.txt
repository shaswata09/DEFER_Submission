However, supplementary relationships apply to same-side interior or same-side exterior angles—not corresponding ones.  
   - **Key Concept:** *Corresponding Angles Postulate*: If lines are parallel, then corresponding angles are congruent.

2. **Question: "In the diagram, ∠3 and ∠5 are same-side interior angles formed by parallel lines and a transversal. If m∠3 = x and m∠5 = 2x, find x."**

   - **Explanation:** Same-side interior angles are supplementary when lines are parallel. So:  
     x + 2x = 180° → 3x = 180° → x = 60°  
     The calculation is correct, but the student failed to state the theorem used. In geometric proofs, both reasoning and computation are essential. Always cite the relevant postulate or theorem (e.g., *Same-Side Interior Angles Theorem*).  
   - **Key Concept:** *Same-Side Interior Angles Theorem*: If two parallel lines are cut by a transversal, then same-side interior angles are supplementary.

SSA (Side-Side-Angle) is not a valid congruence criterion because it does not guarantee unique triangle congruence (it can produce ambiguous cases). However, when the angle is *included* between the two sides, the correct criterion is SAS. The inclusion of the angle is crucial.  
   - **Key Concept:** *SAS Congruence Postulate*: If two sides and the included angle of one triangle are congruent to those of another, the triangles are congruent.
