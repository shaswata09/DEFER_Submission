**Google (GOOG) – Q2 2024 Earnings Summary:**

Alphabet (Google) delivered solid results in Q2 2024, fueled by advertising strength and continued cost discipline.

- **Net Income:** $23.7 billion, a significant 18.6% increase from Q2 2023.
