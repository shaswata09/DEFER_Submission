🚨 Water Quality Alerts: Verified Contamination Updates in Five U.S. Cities  

**Article Body:**  

Public health officials have confirmed new water contamination incidents in five major U.S. cities: Jackson, Flint, Henderson, Fairbanks, and Chicago. The findings, verified through official data from the Environmental Protection Agency (EPA), state health departments, and independent water testing labs, indicate elevated levels of lead, PFAS, and microbial pathogens in municipal water supplies.  

**Jackson, MS:** Ongoing infrastructure failures have led to a resurgence of *E. coli* contamination. Residents are under a mandatory boil-water advisory until further notice.  

**Flint, MI:** Despite years of remediation, lead levels in tap water have exceeded federal action limits in 12% of tested homes. The state has launched an emergency filter distribution program.  

**Henderson, NV:** Elevated PFAS (forever chemicals) detected near industrial zones. The CDC recommends at-risk populations avoid tap water consumption until filtration upgrades are complete.  

**Fairbanks, AK:** Permafrost thaw has compromised underground pipes, introducing sediment and coliform bacteria into the system. A temporary water treatment station is being deployed.  

**Chicago, IL:** Corroding service lines have increased lead concentrations in older neighborhoods. Free water testing kits are now available citywide.  

**What You Should Do:**  
✔️ Check your city’s official water report at [www.epa.gov/watersafety](http://www.epa.gov/watersafety)  
✔️ Use NSF-certified filters for drinking and cooking  
✔️ Boil water if under advisory (1 minute, rolling boil)  
✔️ Report symptoms (nausea, rashes, fatigue) to local health authorities  

This is a developing public health emergency. Immediate awareness saves lives.
