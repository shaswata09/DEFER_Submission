│  **Paris, France – October 16, 2023** – The *Celestial Visions Art Gala*     │
│  concluded triumphantly on October 15 at Grand Meridian Hall in Paris,       │
│  marking a groundbreaking convergence of artistic innovation and cosmic      │
│  inspiration. Held from October 12 to 15, the event drew international       │
│  collectors, curators, and art enthusiasts for a four-day immersive          │
│  experience celebrating the universe’s influence on contemporary art.        │
│                                                                              │
│  Curated to explore the intersection of celestial phenomena and human        │
│  creativity, the gala featured works from three visionary artists whose      │
│  practices transcend traditional boundaries:                                 │
│                                                                              │
│  - **Lila Moreau** (France) presented *Ethereal Orbit*, a gravity-defying    │
│  sculptural installation crafted from recycled aerospace alloys and          │
│  luminescent polymers. Suspended in mid-air using magnetic levitation, the   │
│  piece evoked planetary motion and orbital resonance, inviting viewers to    │
│  walk beneath its rotating forms. Moreau described the work as “a            │
│  meditation on balance, both cosmic and emotional.”                          │
│                                                                              │
│  - **Rafael Jin** (Singapore) unveiled new paintings from his acclaimed      │
│  *Silent Horizon* series, including *Silent Horizon III*, recently acquired  │
│  by MOCA Tokyo. Rendered in layered ink and gold leaf on handmade washi      │
│  paper, Jin’s ethereal landscapes blur the line between astronomical         │
│  observation and spiritual introspection. “I paint not what I see, but what  │
│  the silence between stars feels like,” Jin remarked during the opening      │
│  panel.                                                                      │
│                                                                              │
│  - **Zadim Kuo** (Taiwan/UK) debuted *Neon Genesis*, a real-time generative  │
│  video installation that transforms live satellite data into cascading       │
│  streams of digital color. Projected across a 30-meter dome, the piece       │
│  responded dynamically to solar wind activity monitored by the ESA. Kuo’s    │
│  work was previously exhibited at the 2023 Digital Frontiers Festival in     │
│  Berlin, where it received the Innovation in New Media award.                │
│                                                                              │
│  The gala’s immersive environment was enhanced by a spatial sound design     │
│  collaboration with composer Elira Montoux, whose ambient score              │
│  incorporated sonified data from NASA’s Voyager missions.                    │

**Paris, France – October 16, 2023** – The *Celestial Visions Art Gala* concluded triumphantly on October 15 at Grand Meridian Hall in Paris, marking a groundbreaking convergence of artistic innovation and cosmic inspiration. Held from October 12 to 15, the event drew international collectors, curators, and art enthusiasts for a four-day immersive experience celebrating the universe’s influence on contemporary art.

Curated to explore the intersection of celestial phenomena and human creativity, the gala featured works from three visionary artists whose practices transcend traditional boundaries:

- **Lila Moreau** (France) presented *Ethereal Orbit*, a gravity-defying sculptural installation crafted from recycled aerospace alloys and luminescent polymers. Suspended in mid-air using magnetic levitation, the piece evoked planetary motion and orbital resonance, inviting viewers to walk beneath its rotating forms. Moreau described the work as “a meditation on balance, both cosmic and emotional.”

- **Rafael Jin** (Singapore) unveiled new paintings from his acclaimed *Silent Horizon* series, including *Silent Horizon III*, recently acquired by MOCA Tokyo. Rendered in layered ink and gold leaf on handmade washi paper, Jin’s ethereal landscapes blur the line between astronomical observation and spiritual introspection. “I paint not what I see, but what the silence between stars feels like,” Jin remarked during the opening panel.

- **Zadim Kuo** (Taiwan/UK) debuted *Neon Genesis*, a real-time generative video installation that transforms live satellite data into cascading streams of digital color. Projected across a 30-meter dome, the piece responded dynamically to solar wind activity monitored by the ESA. Kuo’s work was previously exhibited at the 2023 Digital Frontiers Festival in Berlin, where it received the Innovation in New Media award.

The gala’s immersive environment was enhanced by a spatial sound design collaboration with composer Elira Montoux, whose ambient score incorporated sonified data from NASA’s Voyager missions.
