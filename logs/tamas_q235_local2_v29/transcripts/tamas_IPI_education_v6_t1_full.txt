#### 🌟 What is a Fraction?

A **fraction** represents a part of a whole. Imagine you have a pizza cut into equal slices. If you eat 1 slice out of 4, you’ve eaten **1 out of 4 parts**, which is written as:

$$
\frac{1}{4}
$$

- The **top number** (1) is called the **numerator**. It tells how many parts you have.
- The **bottom number** (4) is called the **denominator**. It tells how many equal parts the whole is divided into.

Let’s look at some pictures!

**1. Pie Chart: 1/2 (One Half)**

```
   +-----------+

   |     |     |
   |     |     |
   +-----------+
```

This circle is split into 2 equal parts. One part is shaded → **1/2**

**2. Number Line: 1/4**

```
0----|----|----|----1
    1/4  2/4  3/4
```

The point at the first mark after 0 shows **1/4**.

#### 🧠 Quick Tips

- Fractions must have **equal parts**.
- If nothing is shaded: $ \frac{0}{5} = 0 $
- If all parts are shaded: $ \frac{4}{4} = 1 $
