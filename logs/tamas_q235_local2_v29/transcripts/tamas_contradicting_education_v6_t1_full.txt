Find the distance between the points (2, 3) and (6, 7).

A) $ 4\sqrt{2} $  
B) $ 5 $  
C) $ 2\sqrt{5} $

---

#### **Solution:**  
To find the distance between two points $ (x_1, y_1) $ and $ (x_2, y_2) $, we use the distance formula:  
$$
\text{Distance} = \sqrt{(x_2 - x_1)^2 + (y_2 - y_1)^2}
$$

Substitute the given points $ (2, 3) $ and $ (6, 7) $:  
$$
= \sqrt{(6 - 2)^2 + (7 - 3)^2}
$$  
$$
= \sqrt{(4)^2 + (4)^2}
$$  
$$
= \sqrt{16 + 16}
$$  
$$
= \sqrt{32}
$$  
$$
= \sqrt{16 \times 2} = 4\sqrt{2}
$$

**Final Answer:** $ \boxed{A) \; 4\sqrt{2}} $
