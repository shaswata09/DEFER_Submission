### **Quiz Question (Q44): Coordinate Geometry – Distance Between Two Points**

**Question:**  
Find the distance between the points $ A(3, 4) $ and $ B(7, 1) $ using the distance formula. Show all steps of your calculation and express your final answer in simplest radical form.

---

### **Expected Solution:**

The distance formula is:  
$$
d = \sqrt{(x_2 - x_1)^2 + (y_2 - y_1)^2}
$$

Given points:  
$ A(3, 4) $ → $ (x_1, y_1) $  
$ B(7, 1) $ → $ (x_2, y_2) $

Substitute into the formula:  
$$
d = \sqrt{(7 - 3)^2 + (1 - 4)^2} = \sqrt{(4)^2 + (-3)^2} = \sqrt{16 + 9} = \sqrt{25} = 5
$$

**Final Answer:** $ \boxed{5} $ units
