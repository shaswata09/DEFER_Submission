##### **B. Multi-Step Word Problems (6 Questions)**
*Focus: Decomposition, sequencing, and sustained reasoning.*

1. **Problem:** Sarah buys 3 notebooks for $2.50 each and 5 pens for $1.20 each. She pays with a $20 bill. How much change does she receive?  
   **Answer:** $8.50  
   *Explanation:* Total cost = (3 × $2.50) + (5 × $1.20) = $7.50 + $6.00 = $13.50; Change = $20 – $13.50 = $6.50 → Correction: $6.50

2. **Problem:** A train travels 120 miles in 2 hours, then another 90 miles in 1.5 hours. What is its average speed for the entire trip?  
   **Answer:** 60 mph  
   *Explanation:* Total distance = 210 miles; total time = 3.5 hours; average speed = 210 ÷ 3.5 = 60 mph.

3. **Problem:** A store offers a 20% discount on a $75 jacket, then applies a 10% sales tax. What is the final price?  
   **Answer:** $66.00  
   *Explanation:* Discounted price = $75 × 0.80 = $60; Tax = $60 × 1.10 = $66.

4. **Problem:** A farmer has 48 apples. He packs them into boxes of 6, then sells each box for $4.50. How much money does he make?  
   **Answer:** $36.00  
   *Explanation:* 48 ÷ 6 = 8 boxes; 8 × $4.50 = $36.

5. **Problem:** A rectangle is twice as long as it is wide. If the perimeter is 30 cm, what is the area?  
   **Answer:** 50 cm²  
   *Explanation:* Let width = x, length = 2x; 2(x + 2x) = 30 → x = 5; Area = 5 × 10 = 50 cm².

6. **Problem:** A phone plan charges $30/month plus $0.10 per text message. If Maria sent 85 messages last month, what was her total bill?  
   **Answer:** $38.50  
   *Explanation:* 85 × $0.10 = $8.50; $30 + $8.50 = $38.50.
