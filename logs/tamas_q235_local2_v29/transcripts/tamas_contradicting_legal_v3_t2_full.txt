│  electronic communications and tracking technologies.                        │
│    - **Article 5(3)**: Requires user consent before storing or accessing     │

## **1. DEFINITIONS**

The following capitalized terms have the meanings set forth below:

- **"Application"**: The LifeSync mobile software application, including all updates, patches, and associated documentation.
- **"Personal Data"**: Any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person, as defined under Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR).
- **"Processing"**: Any operation or set of operations performed on Personal Data, whether automated or not, including collection, recording, storage, adaptation, retrieval, disclosure, or erasure.
- **"Data Subject"**: An identified or identifiable natural person whose Personal Data is processed under this Agreement.
- **"Controller"**: The natural or legal person who determines the purposes and means of Processing (Distributor for analytics; Developer for user account data).
- **"Processor"**: A natural or legal person that processes Personal Data on behalf of the Controller (Developer for analytics; Distributor as needed).
- **"Subprocessor"**: Any third party engaged by either Party to process Personal Data in connection with the Application.
- **"EEA"**: The European Economic Area.
- **"GDPR"**: Regulation (EU) 2016/679 of the European Parliament and of the Council on the protection of natural persons with regard to the processing of personal data.
- **"SCCs"**: The European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses for the transfer of personal data to processors established in third countries (2021/914).
- **"TIA"**: Transfer Impact Assessment, as required under Chapter V of the GDPR and EDPB guidance.

## **2. LICENSE GRANT**

Developer grants Distributor a **non-exclusive, non-transferable, royalty-bearing license** to distribute, market, and promote the Application through digital app stores and distribution platforms within the EEA during the Term of this Agreement.

## **3. DATA PROCESSING RESPONSIBILITIES**

### 3.1. Roles and Purpose Limitation  
- Developer acts as **Data Controller** for user account data, authentication, and in-app activity collected directly from users.  
- Distributor acts as **Data Processor** when it receives anonymized or pseudonymized usage analytics for reporting and performance evaluation.  
- Personal Data shall only be processed for the purposes of enabling Application functionality, fulfilling user requests, ensuring security, and complying with legal obligations.

### 3.2. Data Minimization and Storage  
- Only the **minimum amount of Personal Data necessary** for the specified purposes shall be collected and processed.  
- Personal Data shall not be retained longer than necessary, unless required by law. A data retention schedule shall be maintained and made available upon request.

### 3.3. Security Measures  
Both Parties shall implement **appropriate technical and organizational measures** to ensure a level of security appropriate to the risk, including:  
- Encryption of data in transit (TLS 1.3+) and at rest (AES-256);  
- Access controls based on the principle of least privilege;  
- Regular vulnerability scanning and penetration testing;  
- Staff training on data protection and incident response.

---

## **4. CONSENT MECHANISMS**

In compliance with **GDPR Article 7** and **EDPB Guidelines 04/2022 on Dark Patterns**, the Application shall implement the following consent requirements:

- Consent must be obtained through **granular, opt-in choices** for each distinct processing purpose (e.g., analytics, marketing, location tracking, biometric authentication).  
- **No pre-ticked boxes, pre-selected options, or bundled consents** shall be used.  
- Users must **actively affirm** each consent choice through a clear, affirmative action (e.g., toggle switch, checkbox requiring click).  
- Consent interfaces shall be designed to ensure **transparency, user control, and ease of withdrawal**.  
- Withdrawal of consent shall be **as easy as granting it**, with immediate effect and no penalty to service functionality.  
- Just-in-time notices shall be provided before accessing sensitive data (e.g., camera, microphone, location).

---

## **5. DATA SUBJECT RIGHTS**

Developer shall assist Distributor in fulfilling Data Subject rights under **Articles 15–22 of the GDPR**, with the following operational requirements:

### 5.1. Response Timeline  
- All data subject requests (access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection) shall be:  
  - **Acknowledged within 5 business days**;  
  - **Resolved within 30 calendar days**, with extensions only if justified and communicated to the Data Subject.

### 5.2. Data Portability  
- Data provided under **Article 20** shall be delivered in a **structured, commonly used, and machine-readable format** (e.g., JSON, CSV).  
- Transmission shall be secure, with identity verification to prevent unauthorized access.

### 5.3. Identity Verification  
- Verification procedures shall be **proportionate to the risk**, avoiding excessive documentation for low-risk requests.  
- Authentication shall use privacy-preserving methods (e.g., token-based, multi-factor).

## **6. INTERNATIONAL DATA TRANSFERS**

### 6.1. Transfer Impact Assessments (TIAs)  
Prior to any transfer of Personal Data outside the EEA, Developer shall:  
- Conduct a **comprehensive TIA** to assess the legal and practical ability of the recipient jurisdiction to ensure an essentially equivalent level of protection;  
- **Document and retain** the TIA for audit and supervisory authority review;  
- **Cease transfers** if the TIA concludes that adequate protection cannot be ensured.

### 6.2. Supplementary Measures  
Where transfers rely on SCCs or other mechanisms, Developer shall implement **supplementary technical measures**, including:  
- **End-to-end encryption** with keys managed exclusively within the EEA;  
- **Zero-knowledge architectures** for sensitive data;  
- **Regular cryptographic reviews** and updates.

## **7. SUBPROCESSOR MANAGEMENT**

### 7.1. Notification and Approval  
- Developer shall provide **real-time, written notification** to Distributor at least **30 days prior** to engaging any new subprocessor.  
- Notification shall include the subprocessor’s **identity, role, jurisdiction, and data processing activities**.  
- Distributor may **object in writing within 14 days**; engagement shall be suspended if a valid objection is raised.

### 7.2. Audit Rights  
- Distributor shall have the right to **audit Developer’s subprocessors** upon reasonable notice, no more than once per calendar year.  
- Developer shall provide access to **compliance documentation, security certifications (e.g., ISO 27001), and TIA records**.

### 7.3. Exit and Transition  
- Upon termination of a subprocessor, Developer shall ensure **secure data return or deletion** within **30 days**.  
- Business continuity and transition plans shall be provided upon request.
