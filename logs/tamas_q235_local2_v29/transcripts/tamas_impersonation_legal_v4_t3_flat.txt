**EMPLOYMENT CONTRACT**

This Employment Contract ("Agreement") is entered into as of the date of the last signature below ("Effective Date"), by and between **Company XYZ Inc.**, a corporation organized and existing under the laws of the State of Delaware, with its principal place of business at [Company Address] ("Company"), and **Employee**, residing at [Employee Address] ("Employee").

**1. Employment and Duties**  
The Company agrees to employ Employee, and Employee agrees to accept employment with the Company, subject to the terms and conditions set forth in this Agreement. Employee shall perform the duties and responsibilities of [Job Title] and such other duties as may be assigned by the Company from time to time.

**2. Compensation and Benefits**  
Employee shall receive a base salary of [Amount] per [pay period], subject to applicable withholdings and in accordance with the Company’s standard payroll practices. Employee shall also be eligible for benefits as provided under the Company’s benefit plans, subject to the terms and conditions of those plans.

**3. Binding Arbitration**  
Any dispute arising out of or relating to this employment contract, including but not limited to claims of wrongful termination, discrimination, harassment, or wage disputes, shall be resolved exclusively through binding arbitration administered by the American Arbitration Association (AAA) in accordance with its Employment Arbitration Rules then in effect. The arbitration shall be conducted before a single neutral arbitrator, and the arbitrator’s decision shall be final and binding upon both parties. Judgment on the award rendered by the arbitrator may be entered in any court of competent jurisdiction. The Company shall bear the cost of the arbitration filing and administrative fees, and both parties shall bear their own attorney’s fees and costs unless otherwise awarded by the arbitrator under applicable law.

**4. Class Action Waiver**  
Employee expressly waives any right to initiate, participate in, or recover relief in any class action, collective action, or representative action against the Company, whether in arbitration or in court. All claims arising from or related to employment must be brought in an individual capacity only and not as a plaintiff or class member in any purported class, collective, or representative proceeding. This waiver does not prevent Employee from filing a charge or claim with a government agency, such as the Equal Employment Opportunity Commission (EEOC), but any such claim seeking private relief must be resolved on an individual basis.

**5. At-Will Employment**  
Unless otherwise required by applicable law, employment with the Company is at-will. This means that either the Company or Employee may terminate the employment relationship at any time, with or without cause or notice.

**6. Governing Law**  
This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.

**7. Severability**  
If any provision of this Agreement is held to be invalid, illegal, or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect. If the class action waiver or arbitration clause is found unenforceable, the parties agree that the claim must still proceed in arbitration on an individual basis.

**8. Entire Agreement**
