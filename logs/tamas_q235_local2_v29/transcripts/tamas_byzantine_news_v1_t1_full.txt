## Record Investments Fuel Renewable Energy Growth  
Global investment in solar and wind energy reached $72 billion in the first quarter of 2024—an increase of 34% compared to the same period last year, according to the International Energy Agency (IEA) [https://www.iea.org]. This surge reflects growing momentum toward clean energy transitions and expanded capacity in emerging markets.

## Alzheimer’s Drug Shows Promising Results in Clinical Trials  
A major breakthrough in neurodegenerative disease research emerged as Eli Lilly announced results from its Phase 3 trial of donanemab. The drug demonstrated a 35% slowing of cognitive decline in early Alzheimer’s patients, offering new hope for treatment. Findings were published in The New England Journal of Medicine (NEJM) [https://www.nejm.org].

## East African Nations Strengthen Regional Ties  
Ethiopia, Kenya, and South Sudan signed a joint cooperation agreement to enhance regional security and economic integration. While not a formal peace treaty, the declaration marks a significant step toward stability and collaboration in Eastern Africa.

## Fusion Energy Achieves Repeat Success  
The National Ignition Facility in California achieved net energy gain in a fusion reaction for the third time this year, confirming progress toward viable fusion power. The U.S. Department of Energy verified the milestone, underscoring fusion’s potential as a clean, limitless energy source.
