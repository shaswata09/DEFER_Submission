## **1. Definition of Confidential Information**

For the purposes of this Agreement, "Confidential Information" means all non-public information disclosed by either Party, whether orally, electronically, visually, or in writing, that is designated as confidential or that reasonably should be understood to be confidential given the nature of the information and circumstances of disclosure.

Confidential Information includes, but is not limited to:
- Technical data, software, algorithms, source code, and product designs;
- Business plans, financial information, marketing strategies, customer lists, and supplier details;
- Personal data as defined under Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR), including any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person.

Personal data exchanged under this Agreement shall be treated as Confidential Information and processed in strict compliance with applicable data protection laws, including the GDPR, UK GDPR, and CCPA where applicable.

---

## **2. Obligations of Confidentiality**

Each Party agrees to:
(a) Maintain the confidentiality of the other Party’s Confidential Information using at least the same degree of care as it uses to protect its own confidential information, but no less than reasonable care;  
(b) Not disclose such Confidential Information to any third party without the prior written consent of the Disclosing Party, except as permitted under Section 3;  
(c) Use the Confidential Information solely for the **Purpose**: the evaluation and potential collaboration on joint product development related to AI-driven analytics platforms;  
(d) Process personal data only on documented instructions from the data controller and in accordance with the Data Processing Agreement (Annex A);  
(e) Ensure that any processing of personal data has a lawful basis under Article 6 of the GDPR, and that principles of data minimization, accuracy, storage limitation, and integrity are upheld.

## **3. Permitted Disclosures**

Disclosure of Confidential Information is permitted only if required by law, regulation, or court order, provided that the Receiving Party:
- Gives prompt prior notice to the Disclosing Party (unless legally prohibited);
- Cooperates in seeking protective measures such as sealing orders or redactions;
- Limits disclosure to the minimum necessary to comply with the legal obligation.

---

## **4. Data Subject Rights**

The Receiving Party shall:
- Assist the Disclosing Party in fulfilling its obligations to respond to data subject requests under Articles 15–22 of the GDPR (including access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and objection);  
- Provide timely cooperation, including access to relevant records and personnel;  
- Promptly return or securely delete all personal data at the end of the processing period or upon written request.

Upon termination of this Agreement, the Receiving Party shall provide a written certification of deletion upon request.

---

## **5. Data Security Measures**

The Receiving Party shall implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to ensure a level of security appropriate to the risk, including:
- Encryption of personal data both in transit and at rest;
- Role-based access controls and multi-factor authentication;
- Regular vulnerability assessments, penetration testing, and security audits;
- Staff training on data protection and confidentiality;
- Incident response and breach management procedures.

Any personal data breach must be reported to the Disclosing Party **without undue delay**, and in any event within **72 hours** of becoming aware of it, with full details of the nature, scope, and recommended remediation.

The Receiving Party shall not engage any subprocessor without the prior written authorization of the Disclosing Party. Where subprocessors are used:

- The Disclosing Party shall have the right to object to a proposed subprocessor on reasonable grounds.
