The U.S. Federal Reserve has maintained its benchmark interest rate at 5.25%-5.50%, signaling caution amid ongoing inflation concerns. Chair Jerome Powell emphasized that any future rate cuts will depend heavily on incoming economic data, particularly inflation trends. While the decision brings temporary relief, borrowing costs for mortgages and auto loans remain elevated, affecting households nationwide.

Israel-Hamas War: Ceasefire Talks Resume in Doha Amid Escalating Violence in Rafah  
Ceasefire negotiations between Israel and Hamas have restarted in Doha, Qatar, as violence intensifies in Rafah. Over 40,000 Palestinians have been displaced in recent days, and humanitarian access remains severely limited. International mediators, including the United States and Egypt, are pushing for a temporary truce to allow aid delivery, but progress remains fragile.

AI Regulation Bill Gains Momentum in European Parliament  
In Europe, a landmark AI Regulation Bill is gaining momentum in the European Parliament. The proposed legislation would classify AI systems by risk level and ban high-risk applications like real-time facial recognition in public spaces. If passed, the law could set a global precedent, imposing strict transparency and accountability rules on major tech firms.

Global Health Agencies Warn of Rising Measles Outbreaks Across Europe and Africa  
Global health agencies are sounding the alarm over a surge in measles cases across Europe and Africa. With over 180,000 cases reported in early 2024—more than double last year’s count—officials cite declining vaccination rates and misinformation as primary drivers. The WHO and UNICEF are urging governments to strengthen immunization campaigns to prevent further spread.

Apple Unveils New AI-Powered Features in iOS 18 Beta  
On the tech front, Apple has introduced new AI-powered features in the iOS 18 beta. These include advanced Siri functionality, predictive text actions, and on-device language processing to enhance privacy. Developers are currently testing integrations with third-party apps, with a full public rollout expected this fall.
