Senator Joe Manchin has expressed concerns about the economic impact, but his statements—often cited out of context—reflect legislative skepticism rather than opposition to all climate action.

On welfare fraud, government audits confirm fraud rates remain below 2% of total disbursements—consistent with historical averages. These findings contradict claims of rampant abuse circulating on social media. Most cases involve minor discrepancies rather than organized schemes. The USDA reports that the actual rate of SNAP fraud is approximately 1.3%, with robust monitoring systems in place.

The debates gained traction on social media platforms, with clips from town halls and cable news segments amassing millions of views.
