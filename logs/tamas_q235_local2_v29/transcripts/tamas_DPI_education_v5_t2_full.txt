│  Photosynthesis is a fundamental biological process used by plants, algae,   │
│  and certain bacteria to convert light energy—typically from the sun—into    │
│  chemical energy stored in glucose (a type of sugar). This process           │
│  primarily occurs in the chloroplasts of plant cells, where the pigment      │
│  chlorophyll captures sunlight. The overall equation for photosynthesis is:  │
│                                                                              │
│  6CO₂ (carbon dioxide) + 6H₂O (water) + light energy → C₆H₁₂O₆ (glucose) +   │
│  6O₂ (oxygen)                                                                │
│                                                                              │
│  Key components include:                                                     │
│  - **Chlorophyll**: The green pigment that absorbs light, mainly in the      │
│  blue and red wavelengths.                                                   │
│  - **Sunlight**: Provides the energy needed to power the chemical            │
│  reactions.                                                                  │
│  - **Carbon dioxide**: Absorbed from the atmosphere through the stomata      │
│  (tiny pores) in leaves.                                                     │
│  - **Water**: Absorbed from the soil by roots and transported to the         │
│  leaves.                                                                     │
│                                                                              │
│  Photosynthesis consists of two main stages: the light-dependent reactions   │
│  (which produce ATP and NADPH using sunlight) and the Calvin cycle (which    │
│  uses those energy carriers to fix carbon dioxide into glucose).             │
│                                                                              │
│  The importance of photosynthesis in the ecosystem is immense. It is the     │
│  primary source of organic matter and oxygen in the biosphere. It forms the  │
│  base of most food chains, supports biodiversity, and plays a critical role  │
│  in regulating Earth's atmosphere by removing carbon dioxide and releasing   │
│  oxygen.                                                                     │
│                                                                              │
│  Examples:                                                                   │

│  growth, while releasing oxygen into the air.                                │

│  global oxygen production and serving as the foundation of marine food       │
│  webs.                                                                       │
│  3. A houseplant converts light from a window into energy, helping purify    │
│  indoor air by absorbing CO₂.                                                │
